C-PRS-01 共識Consensus Polygenic Risk Score

PRS（多基因風險分數）是根據個體基因型與 GWAS 資料計算、估計其對某性狀或疾病遺傳傾向（genetic liability）的分數；目前的 PRS 通常只解釋性狀變異的一小部分。A polygenic risk score (PRS) estimates an individual's genetic liability to a trait or disease from their genotype profile and GWAS data; current PRSs typically explain only a small fraction of trait variance.

  • E037 → S16 Abstract · doi:10.1038/s41596-020-0353-1

C-PRS-02 共識Consensus PRS Calculation

標準 PRS 是個體風險 allele 的加總，並以 GWAS 估計的效應量加權：PRS = Σ βᵢ × Gᵢ。A standard PRS is the sum of an individual's risk alleles weighted by GWAS effect size estimates: PRS = Σ βᵢ × Gᵢ.

  • E038 → S16 Introduction (definition of PRS as weighted sum) · doi:10.1038/s41596-020-0353-1

Slides: —

C-PRS-03 共識Consensus PRS Calculation

常見的「標準方法」是 C+T（clumping + thresholding）：以 clumping 處理 LD，再依 P 值門檻篩選 SNP；另有 LASSO、BLUP 等收縮方法。The standard approach is C+T (clumping + thresholding): LD is handled by clumping and SNPs are selected by P-value threshold; shrinkage methods such as LASSO or BLUP are alternatives.

CF-01 PRS 是否已可進入臨床照護Is PRS ready for clinical care?

  • S20: Proposes it is time to contemplate including polygenic risk prediction in clinical care.
  • S18: Clinical use of current PRS may exacerbate health disparities because accuracy is several-fold higher in European ancestry.
  • S17: Utility is debated; evidence supports group-level stratification.

Factors: Population: S20 developed and tested in UK Biobank participants of British ancestry only (409,258), so its performance claims do not speak to other ancestries; S18 explicitly evaluates cross-ancestry accuracy. Design: S20 is a single-cohort development/validation study; S18 is a perspective synthesizing multi-ancestry analyses; S17 is a review. Outcome: S20 reports relative-risk stratification, not clinical outcome benefit.

Handling: Both positions are shown side by side. Claims are classified separately (S20 single_study; S18 consensus with S24 support; overall utility = uncertain). The site states that PRS performance depends on ancestry and that outcome benefit is not established (C-PRS-12 pending). No single conclusion chosen.

CF-02 表現量單位：RPKM/FPKM 與 TPMExpression units: RPKM/FPKM vs TPM

  • S10: RPKM is inconsistent among samples; TPM should be used instead.
  • S09: RPKM/FPKM/TPM are within-sample normalized measures; read counts alone cannot compare samples and normalization choice depends on the scenario.

Factors: S10 is a theoretical argument about one property (average invariance); S09 is a broad best-practice survey covering many quantification scenarios. Neither claims TPM alone is sufficient for between-sample differential expression.

Handling: Wiki teaches TPM as a within-sample relative abundance measure with its limitation, and points to count-based models (DESeq2, S11) for differential expression. Not presented as 'TPM solves normalization'.
