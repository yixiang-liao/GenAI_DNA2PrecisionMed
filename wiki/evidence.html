C-MOL-01 共識Consensus Deoxyribonucleic Acid

DNA 是攜帶遺傳資訊的分子，由 A、C、G、T 四種鹼基組成；A 與 T 配對、C 與 G 配對。DNA is the molecule that carries genetic information; it uses four bases (A, C, G, T), with A pairing with T and C pairing with G.

  • E001 → S01 Glossary entry "Deoxyribonucleic Acid (DNA)" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “Deoxyribonucleic acid (abbreviated DNA) is the molecule that carries genetic information for the development and functioning of an organism. … Attached to each sugar is one of four bases: adenine (A), cytosine (C), guanine (G) or thymine (T). The two strands are connected by chemical bonds between the bases: adenine bonds with thymine, and cytosine bonds with guanine.”

C-MOL-02 共識Consensus Ribonucleic Acid

RNA 與 DNA 結構相似，但多為單股、骨架使用核糖（ribose），並以尿嘧啶（U）取代胸腺嘧啶（T）；細胞中有 mRNA、rRNA、tRNA 等，部分 RNA 參與基因表現調控。RNA resembles DNA but is most often single-stranded, uses ribose instead of deoxyribose, and uses uracil (U) instead of thymine (T); cells contain mRNA, rRNA and tRNA, and some RNAs regulate gene expression.

  • E002 → S01 Glossary entry "Ribonucleic Acid (RNA)" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “Unlike DNA, however, RNA is most often single-stranded. An RNA molecule has a backbone made of alternating phosphate groups and the sugar ribose, rather than the deoxyribose found in DNA. … Attached to each sugar is one of four bases: adenine (A), uracil (U), cytosine (C) or guanine (G). Different types of RNA exist in cells: messenger RNA (mRNA), ribosomal RNA (rRNA) and transfer RNA (tRNA). In addition, some RNAs are involved in regulating gene expression.”

C-MOL-03 共識Consensus Chromosome

染色體是由蛋白質與一條 DNA 分子組成的線狀結構；人類有 22 對體染色體與 1 對性染色體，共 46 條。A chromosome is a threadlike structure of protein and a single DNA molecule; humans have 22 pairs of autosomes and one pair of sex chromosomes, 46 in total.

  • E003 → S01 Glossary entry "Chromosome" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “Chromosomes are threadlike structures made of protein and a single molecule of DNA that serve to carry the genomic information from cell to cell. … Humans have 22 pairs of numbered chromosomes (autosomes) and one pair of sex chromosomes (XX or XY), for a total of 46.”

Slides: s03

C-MOL-04 共識Consensus Gene

基因是遺傳的基本單位，多數基因編碼蛋白質；人類約有 20,000 個蛋白質編碼基因。The gene is the basic unit of inheritance; most genes code for proteins, and humans have approximately 20,000 protein-coding genes.

  • E004 → S01 Glossary entry "Gene" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “The gene is considered the basic unit of inheritance. … Most genes code for specific proteins, or segments of proteins, which have differing functions within the body. Humans have approximately 20,000 protein-coding genes.”

C-MOL-05 共識Consensus Locus

Locus（基因座）是基因體中的一個實體位置，例如一個基因或其他 DNA 片段，類似街道地址；複數為 loci。A locus is a physical site in a genome, such as a gene or another DNA segment, somewhat like a street address; the plural is loci.

  • E005 → S01 Glossary entry "Locus" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “A locus, as related to genomics, is a physical site or location within a genome (such as a gene or another DNA segment of interest), somewhat like a street address. The plural of locus is loci.”

Slides: s04

C-MOL-06 共識Consensus Allele

Allele（對偶基因）是同一基因體位置上的不同 DNA 序列版本；每個人在該位置從父母各得一個 allele，兩者相同為同型合子（homozygous），不同為異型合子（heterozygous）。An allele is one of two or more versions of DNA sequence at a given genomic location; an individual inherits two alleles, one from each parent — identical alleles make the individual homozygous, different alleles heterozygous.

  • E006 → S01 Glossary entry "Allele" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “An allele is one of two or more versions of DNA sequence (a single base or a segment of bases) at a given genomic location. An individual inherits two alleles, one from each parent, for any given genomic location where such variation exists. If the two alleles are the same, the individual is homozygous for that allele. If the alleles are different, the individual is heterozygous.”

Slides: s05

C-MOL-07 共識Consensus Single Nucleotide Polymorphism

SNP（單核苷酸多態性）是發生在單一鹼基位置的基因體變異，研究者探討 SNP 是否及如何影響健康、疾病、藥物反應與其他性狀。A single nucleotide polymorphism (SNP) is a genomic variant at a single base position; scientists study if and how SNPs influence health, disease, drug response and other traits.

  • E007 → S01 Glossary entry "Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms (SNPs)" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “A single nucleotide polymorphism (abbreviated SNP, pronounced snip) is a genomic variant at a single base position in the DNA. Scientists study if and how SNPs in a genome influence health, disease, drug response and other traits.”

Slides: s05

C-MOL-08 單一研究Single Study Finding Genetic Variant

1000 Genomes Project 重建了 26 個族群、2,504 人的基因體，共描述超過 8,800 萬個變異（約 8,470 萬個 SNP、360 萬個短插入/刪除與 6 萬個結構變異）。The 1000 Genomes Project reconstructed the genomes of 2,504 individuals from 26 populations and characterized over 88 million variants (84.7 million SNPs, 3.6 million short indels and 60,000 structural variants).

  • E008 → S06 Abstract · doi:10.1038/nature15393
    “Here we report completion of the project, having reconstructed the genomes of 2,504 individuals from 26 populations … in total over 88 million variants (84.7 million single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs), 3.6 million short insertions/deletions (indels), and 60,000 structural variants)”

C-MOL-09 共識Consensus Genotype

Genotype（基因型）是對某一基因座上變異類型的判定；部分基因型會影響個體可觀察的性狀，即 phenotype。A genotype is a scoring of the type of variant present at a given locus; some genotypes contribute to an individual's observable traits, called the phenotype.

  • E009 → S01 Glossary entry "Genotype" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “A genotype is a scoring of the type of variant present at a given location (i.e., a locus) in the genome. … Some genotypes contribute to an individual’s observable traits, called the phenotype.”

C-MOL-10 共識Consensus Phenotype

Phenotype（表現型）是個體可觀察的性狀，例如身高、眼睛顏色與血型；它同時由基因型與環境因素決定。Phenotype refers to an individual's observable traits, such as height, eye colour and blood type, and is determined by both genotype and environmental factors.

  • E010 → S01 Glossary entry "Phenotype" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “Phenotype refers to an individual’s observable traits, such as height, eye color and blood type. A person’s phenotype is determined by both their genomic makeup (genotype) and environmental factors.”

C-MOL-11 單一研究Single Study Finding Genetic Variant

在 1000 Genomes 資料中，大多數變異位點是罕見的（約 6,400 萬個體染色體變異頻率 <0.5%）；但單一個人基因體中觀察到的變異，大多數是常見變異。In the 1000 Genomes data most variant sites are rare (~64 million autosomal variants have frequency <0.5%), yet most variants observed in a single genome are common.

C-SEQ-01 共識Consensus Next-Generation Sequencing

人類基因體計畫完成後，定序技術進步使每百萬鹼基成本下降、已定序基因體數量增加；有些方法追求單位時間內最多鹼基，有些則追求更長的連續讀段以解析結構複雜區域。Since the Human Genome Project, sequencing advances have lowered the cost per megabase and increased the number of sequenced genomes; some approaches maximize bases per unit time while others sequence longer contiguous DNA to resolve structurally complex regions.

  • E011 → S03 Abstract · doi:10.1038/nrg.2016.49
    “Since the completion of the human genome project in 2003, extraordinary progress has been made in genome sequencing technologies, which has led to a decreased cost per megabase and an increase in the number and diversity of sequenced genomes. … Some approaches maximize the number of bases sequenced in the least amount of time … other approaches now aim to sequence longer contiguous pieces of DNA, which are essential for resolving structurally complex regions.”

Slides: s07

C-SEQ-02 不確定Uncertain Next-Generation Sequencing

（截至 2016 年）長讀段定序克服了讀段長度限制，但成本明顯較高、通量較低，限制了其普及。此描述具時效性。(As of 2016) long-read sequencing overcomes read-length limits but was considerably more expensive and lower-throughput, limiting adoption. This statement is time-sensitive.

Time-sensitive: describes the 2016 state of the field; costs and throughput of long-read platforms have changed since.

  • E012 → S03 Main text, introduction (opening section) · doi:10.1038/nrg.2016.49
    “Although long-read sequencing overcomes the length limitation of other NGS approaches, it remains considerably more expensive and has lower throughput than other platforms, limiting the widespread adoption of this technology in favour of less-expensive approaches.”

C-SEQ-03 共識Consensus Whole Exome Sequencing

Exome（外顯子體）是基因體中所有外顯子的序列，代表蛋白質編碼部分，約占人類基因體的 1.5%。The exome is the sequence of all exons in a genome — the protein-coding portion — and is about 1.5% of the human genome.

  • E013 → S01 Glossary entry "Exome" (genome.gov) · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “An exome is the sequence of all the exons in a genome, reflecting the protein-coding portion of a genome. In humans, the exome is about 1.5% of the genome.”

Slides: s08

C-SEQ-04 單一研究Single Study Finding Whole Genome Sequencing

1000 Genomes Project 結合低覆蓋度全基因體定序、深度外顯子定序與高密度微陣列基因分型，說明 WGS、WES 與 array 可互補使用，而非只能擇一。The 1000 Genomes Project combined low-coverage whole-genome sequencing, deep exome sequencing and dense microarray genotyping — WGS, WES and arrays can be used together rather than only one at a time.

  • E014 → S06 Abstract · doi:10.1038/nature15393
    “having reconstructed the genomes of 2,504 individuals from 26 populations using a combination of low-coverage whole-genome sequencing, deep exome sequencing, and dense microarray genotyping.”

C-SEQ-05 共識Consensus Variant Call Format

VCF（Variant Call Format）是儲存 SNP、插入/刪除與結構變異及其註解的通用格式，通常壓縮並建立索引以快速查詢；最初為 1000 Genomes Project 開發。The Variant Call Format (VCF) is a generic format for storing SNPs, indels and structural variants with annotations; it is usually compressed and indexed for fast retrieval and was developed for the 1000 Genomes Project.

  • E015 → S04 Abstract (Summary) · doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btr330
    “The variant call format (VCF) is a generic format for storing DNA polymorphism data such as SNPs, insertions, deletions and structural variants, together with rich annotations. VCF is usually stored in a compressed manner and can be indexed for fast data retrieval of variants from a range of positions on the reference genome. The format was developed for the 1000 Genomes Project”

Slides: s09

C-SEQ-06 共識Consensus Variant Calling

GATK 是為 NGS 資料設計的分析框架，提供覆蓋度計算與 SNP calling 等工具，已用於 1000 Genomes Project 與 TCGA 等大型計畫。GATK is an analysis framework for next-generation sequencing data, providing tools such as coverage calculators and SNP calling, used in large projects including the 1000 Genomes Project and TCGA.

  • E016 → S05 Abstract · doi:10.1101/gr.107524.110
    “Here, we discuss our Genome Analysis Toolkit (GATK), a structured programming framework designed to ease the development of efficient and robust analysis tools for next-generation DNA sequencers … robust, scale-tolerant tools like coverage calculators and single nucleotide polymorphism (SNP) calling. … many of which have already been incorporated into large-scale sequencing projects like the 1000 Genomes Project and The Cancer Genome Atlas.”

Slides: s09

C-SEQ-07 共識Consensus Mutation (Germline / Somatic)

遺傳性（germline）變異由父母傳給子女，存在於幾乎所有細胞；後天（somatic）變異在生命中某時發生，只存在部分細胞，且不會遺傳給下一代。Hereditary (germline) variants are passed from parent to child and are present in virtually every cell; acquired (somatic) variants arise during life, are present only in some cells, and cannot be passed to the next generation.

  • E017 → S02 MedlinePlus page "What is a gene variant and how do variants occur?" · https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/understanding/
    “Inherited (or hereditary) variants are passed from parent to child and are present throughout a person’s life in virtually every cell in the body. These variants are also called germline variants … Non-inherited variants occur at some time during a person’s life and are present only in certain cells, not in every cell in the body. … they are often referred to as somatic variants. These variants cannot be passed to the next generation.”

Slides: s10

C-SEQ-08 單一研究Single Study Finding Reference Genome

T2T Consortium 發表完整的 30.55 億鹼基對人類基因體 T2T-CHM13，補上先前參考基因體缺少的約 8%，除 Y 染色體外所有染色體皆無缺口。The T2T Consortium presented T2T-CHM13, a complete 3.055-billion-base-pair human genome that adds the ~8% missing from earlier references, with gapless assemblies for all chromosomes except Y.

  • E018 → S07 Abstract · doi:10.1126/science.abj6987
    “Addressing the remaining 8% of the genome, the Telomere-to-Telomere (T2T) Consortium presents a complete 3.055 billion-base pair sequence of a human genome, T2T-CHM13, that includes gapless assemblies for all chromosomes except Y”

Slides: s07

C-SEQ-09 共識Consensus Variant Classification (ACMG/AMP)

ACMG/AMP 建議以 pathogenic、likely pathogenic、uncertain significance、likely benign、benign 五級術語描述孟德爾疾病基因中的變異，並依族群、計算、功能、共分離等證據分類。ACMG/AMP recommend five terms — pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain significance, likely benign, benign — for variants in Mendelian disease genes, classified using population, computational, functional and segregation evidence.

  • E019 → S25 Abstract · doi:10.1038/gim.2015.30
    “This report recommends the use of specific standard terminology-"pathogenic," "likely pathogenic," "uncertain significance," "likely benign," and "benign"-to describe variants identified in genes that cause Mendelian disorders. Moreover, this recommendation describes a process for classifying variants into these five categories based on criteria using typical types of variant evidence (e.g., population data, computational data, functional data, segregation data).”

Slides: s10

C-TX-01 共識Consensus RNA Sequencing

RNA-seq 以深度定序技術分析轉錄體；依 2009 年的回顧，它比當時的其他方法（如 microarray）能更精確測量轉錄本及其 isoform 的量。RNA-seq profiles the transcriptome with deep-sequencing technologies; a 2009 review reported that it measures transcript and isoform levels far more precisely than the other methods of that time (e.g., microarrays).

  • E020 → S08 Abstract · doi:10.1038/nrg2484
    “RNA-Seq is a recently developed approach to transcriptome profiling that uses deep-sequencing technologies. … RNA-Seq also provides a far more precise measurement of levels of transcripts and their isoforms than other methods.”

Slides: s12

C-TX-02 共識Consensus RNA Sequencing

沒有一條 RNA-seq 分析流程適用所有情境；主要步驟包含實驗設計、品質控制、比對、定量、差異表現、選擇性剪接、功能分析與 eQTL mapping 等。No single RNA-seq analysis pipeline fits all cases; major steps include experimental design, quality control, alignment, quantification, differential expression, alternative splicing, functional analysis and eQTL mapping.

  • E021 → S09 Abstract · doi:10.1186/s13059-016-0881-8
    “RNA-sequencing (RNA-seq) has a wide variety of applications, but no single analysis pipeline can be used in all cases. We review all of the major steps in RNA-seq data analysis, including experimental design, quality control, read alignment, quantification of gene and transcript levels, visualization, differential gene expression, alternative splicing, functional analysis, gene fusion detection and eQTL mapping.”

Slides: s12

C-TX-03 單一研究Single Study Finding Transcripts Per Million

Wagner 等人指出 RPKM 在不同樣本間不一致，提出修正版 TPM（transcripts per million），使樣本內平均相對莫耳濃度維持不變。Wagner et al. showed that RPKM is inconsistent among samples and proposed TPM (transcripts per million), which respects the invariance of average relative molar concentration.

  • E022 → S10 Abstract · doi:10.1007/s12064-012-0162-3
    “we show that RPKM does not respect this invariance property and thus cannot be an accurate measure of rmc. We propose a slight modification of RPKM that eliminates this inconsistency and call it TPM for transcripts per million.”

Slides: s13

C-TX-04 共識Consensus Gene Expression

單看讀段數不足以比較不同樣本的表現量，因為讀段數受轉錄本長度、總讀段數與定序偏差影響，需要正規化。Read counts alone are not sufficient to compare expression among samples, because they depend on transcript length, total number of reads and sequencing biases; normalization is needed.

  • E023 → S09 Main text, section "Transcript quantification" · doi:10.1186/s13059-016-0881-8
    “Raw read counts alone are not sufficient to compare expression levels among samples, as these values are affected by factors such as transcript length, total number of reads, and sequencing biases. The measure RPKM (reads per kilobase of exon model per million reads) [] is a within-sample normalization method”

Slides: s13

C-TX-05 共識Consensus Differential Gene Expression

差異表現分析面對重複數少、離散計數、動態範圍大與離群值等問題；DESeq2 以收縮估計（shrinkage）穩定 dispersion 與 fold change 的估計。Differential expression analysis faces small replicate numbers, discrete counts, large dynamic range and outliers; DESeq2 uses shrinkage estimation of dispersions and fold changes to stabilize estimates.

  • E024 → S11 Abstract · doi:10.1186/s13059-014-0550-8
    “Small replicate numbers, discreteness, large dynamic range and the presence of outliers require a suitable statistical approach. We present DESeq2, a method for differential analysis of count data, using shrinkage estimation for dispersions and fold changes to improve stability and interpretability of estimates.”

Slides: s14

C-TX-06 共識Consensus Gene Expression Matrix

差異分析的輸入是計數資料，例如 RNA-seq 中每個基因的讀段數，並比較不同實驗條件之間的系統性變化。Differential analysis takes count data — such as read counts per gene in RNA-seq — and tests for systematic changes across experimental conditions.

  • E025 → S11 Abstract · doi:10.1186/s13059-014-0550-8
    “In comparative high-throughput sequencing assays, a fundamental task is the analysis of count data, such as read counts per gene in RNA-seq, for evidence of systematic changes across experimental conditions.”

Slides: s12 s14

RNA-seq 研究已改變我們對真核生物轉錄體範圍與複雜度的認識。Studies using RNA-seq have altered our view of the extent and complexity of eukaryotic transcriptomes.

  • E026 → S08 Abstract · doi:10.1038/nrg2484
    “Studies using this method have already altered our view of the extent and complexity of eukaryotic transcriptomes.”

C-TX-08 共識Consensus Transcripts Per Million

相較 RPKM/FPKM，TPM 校正了轉錄本組成的差異，因此被認為在樣本間較可比。Compared with RPKM/FPKM, TPM normalizes for differences in transcript composition and is considered more comparable between samples.

  • E067 → S09 Main text, "Transcript quantification" · doi:10.1186/s13059-016-0881-8

C-GW-01 共識Consensus Genome-Wide Association Study

GWAS 在大量個體的基因體中檢測數十萬個變異，找出與特定性狀或疾病「統計上相關」的變異。GWAS test hundreds of thousands of genetic variants across many genomes to find those statistically associated with a specific trait or disease.

  • E027 → S12 Abstract · doi:10.1038/s43586-021-00056-9
    “Genome-wide association studies (GWAS) test hundreds of thousands of genetic variants across many genomes to find those statistically associated with a specific trait or disease.”
  • E028 → S01 Glossary entry "Genome-Wide Association Studies (GWAS)" (genome.gov), Definition · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “A genome-wide association study (abbreviated GWAS) is a research approach used to identify genomic variants that are statistically associated with a risk for a disease or a particular trait. The method involves surveying the genomes of many people, looking for genomic variants that occur more frequently in those with a specific disease or trait compared to those without the disease or trait.”

Slides: s15

C-GW-02 共識Consensus Genome-Wide Association Study

GWAS 結果可用於理解性狀生物學、估計遺傳率、計算遺傳相關、臨床風險預測、藥物開發，以及推論風險因子與健康結果之間可能的因果關係。GWAS results are used to gain insight into a phenotype's biology, estimate heritability, calculate genetic correlations, make clinical risk predictions, inform drug development and infer potential causal relationships between risk factors and outcomes.

  • E029 → S12 Abstract · doi:10.1038/s43586-021-00056-9
    “GWAS results have a range of applications, such as gaining insight into a phenotype’s underlying biology, estimating its heritability, calculating genetic correlations, making clinical risk predictions, informing drug development programmes and inferring potential causal relationships between risk factors and health outcomes.”

Slides: s15

C-GW-03 共識Consensus P-value & Genome-wide Significance

GWAS 常用的全基因體顯著閾值為 p < 5 × 10⁻⁸；Visscher 等人（2017）統計約 10,000 個以此標準定義的強關聯。GWAS conventionally use a genome-wide significance threshold of p < 5 × 10⁻⁸; Visscher et al. (2017) counted about 10,000 strong associations defined this way.

  • E030 → S13 Main text, section "General Results" > "Complex Traits Are Highly Polygenic" (also Table 1) · doi:10.1016/j.ajhg.2017.06.005
    “About 10,000 strong associations have been reported between genetic variants and one or more complex traits, 10 where “strong” is defined as statistically significant at the genome-wide p value threshold of 5 × 10 −8”

Slides: s16

C-GW-04 共識Consensus Linkage Disequilibrium

Linkage disequilibrium（LD）是不同基因座上 allele 的非隨機關聯，受重組、天擇、遺傳漂變與突變影響，被用來定位與性狀或疾病相關的基因。Linkage disequilibrium (LD) is the nonrandom association of alleles at different loci; it is shaped by recombination, selection, drift and mutation, and is used to map genes associated with traits and diseases.

  • E031 → S14 Abstract; Introduction (unheaded opening section) · doi:10.1038/nrg2361
    “Linkage disequilibrium — the nonrandom association of alleles at different loci — is a sensitive indicator of the population genetic forces that structure a genome. … how natural selection, genetic drift, recombination and mutation all affect levels of LD”

Slides: s17

C-GW-05 共識Consensus Genome-Wide Association Study

關聯不等於因果：GWAS 找到的顯著 SNP 通常透過 LD 標記附近未知的因果變異，之後仍需搜尋附近直接影響性狀的變異。Association is not causation: a significant GWAS SNP often tags unknown causal variants through LD, and nearby variants that directly contribute to the trait must still be searched for.

  • E032 → S13 Main text, Introduction (power and LD paragraph, before Figure 1) · doi:10.1016/j.ajhg.2017.06.005
    “The statistical power to detect associations between DNA variants and a trait depends on the experimental sample size, the distribution of effect sizes of (unknown) causal genetic variants that are segregating in the population, the frequency of those variants, and the LD between observed genotyped DNA variants and the unknown causal variants.”
  • E033 → S01 Glossary entry "Genome-Wide Association Studies (GWAS)" (genome.gov), Definition and Narration · https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
    “Once such genomic variants are identified, they are typically used to search for nearby variants that contribute directly to the disease or trait. … They inform us of correlation not causation.”

C-GW-06 共識Consensus Effect Size (Beta / Odds Ratio)

C-PRS-01 共識Consensus Polygenic Risk Score

PRS（多基因風險分數）是根據個體基因型與 GWAS 資料計算、估計其對某性狀或疾病遺傳傾向（genetic liability）的分數；目前的 PRS 通常只解釋性狀變異的一小部分。A polygenic risk score (PRS) estimates an individual's genetic liability to a trait or disease from their genotype profile and GWAS data; current PRSs typically explain only a small fraction of trait variance.

C-PRS-02 共識Consensus PRS Calculation

標準 PRS 是個體風險 allele 的加總，並以 GWAS 估計的效應量加權：PRS = Σ βᵢ × Gᵢ。A standard PRS is the sum of an individual's risk alleles weighted by GWAS effect size estimates: PRS = Σ βᵢ × Gᵢ.

  • E038 → S16 Introduction (opening section, before "1 Introduction to Polygenic Risk Scores") · doi:10.1038/s41596-020-0353-1
    “In the standard approach [,–], polygenic risk scores are calculated by computing the sum of risk alleles corresponding to a phenotype of interest in each individual, weighted by the effect size estimate of the most powerful GWAS on the phenotype.”

C-PRS-03 共識Consensus PRS Calculation

常見的「標準方法」是 C+T（clumping + thresholding）：以 clumping 處理 LD，再依 P 值門檻篩選 SNP。另一類方法對 summary statistics 的權重做收縮（如 LDpred，或 lassosum 這類 LASSO 型懲罰）；以個體層級基因型聯合擬合的 BLUP、LASSO 預測模型則是不同的一類。The standard approach is C+T (clumping + thresholding): LD is handled by clumping and SNPs are selected by a P-value threshold. Other methods shrink summary-statistic weights (e.g. LDpred, or LASSO-type penalties as in lassosum); BLUP or LASSO models fitted jointly on individual-level genotypes are a separate class.

  • E039 → S16 Section 3.2 "Controlling for Linkage Disequilibrium"; Section 3.1 "Shrinkage of GWAS effect size estimates"; Section 1 "Introduction to Polygenic Risk Scores" · doi:10.1038/s41596-020-0353-1
    “phenotypic prediction approaches that exploit individual-level data only. In the latter, genotype effect sizes are typically estimated in joint models of multiple variants and prediction performed simultaneously, using approaches such as best linear unbiased prediction (BLUP) [,] or least absolute shrinkage and selection operator (LASSO) [,]. … PRS methods that perform shrinkage of all SNPs [,,,] generally exploit commonly used statistical shrinkage/regularisation techniques, such as LASSO or ridge regression … which in the case of LDpred, for example, includes a parameter for the fraction of causal variants … In the ‘standard approach’ to PRS calculation, option (1) is combined with P -value thresholding and called the C+T ( clumping + thresholding ) method, while option (2) is generally favoured in methods that implement traditional shrinkage methods”
