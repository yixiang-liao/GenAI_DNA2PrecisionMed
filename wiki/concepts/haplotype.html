分子生物學基礎Molecular Biology Foundations · 11/11

單倍型Haplotype

Concept ID: haplotype · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

同一條染色體上、彼此靠近的幾個 allele 常常一起遺傳下去，這一組 allele 就叫 haplotype，像一串綁在一起的鑰匙。

Alleles close together on the same chromosome tend to be inherited as a set; that set is a haplotype — like keys on one keyring.

進階Advanced L2

對同一染色體上的兩個基因座，allele 組合 AB 即為一個 haplotype，p_AB 為 haplotype frequency；LD 量度 D_AB = p_AB − p_A·p_B 便是建立在 haplotype 頻率上。將基因型「定相」（phasing）成 haplotype 是 imputation 與族群遺傳分析的基礎。

For two loci on one chromosome, the allele pair AB is a haplotype with frequency p_AB; the LD measure D_AB = p_AB − p_A·p_B is built on haplotype frequencies. Phasing genotypes into haplotypes underpins imputation and population-genetic analysis.

例子Examples

  • （示意）某人在兩個 SNP 的基因型為 A/G 與 C/T：可能的 haplotype 是 A–C 與 G–T，也可能是 A–T 與 G–C，需要定相才知道。(Illustrative) Genotypes A/G and C/T at two SNPs could come from haplotypes A–C + G–T or A–T + G–C; phasing tells which.
