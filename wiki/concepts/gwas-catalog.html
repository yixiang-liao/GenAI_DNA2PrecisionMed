GWAS CatalogNHGRI-EBI GWAS Catalog

Concept ID: gwas-catalog · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

GWAS Catalog 是一個公開的「關聯結果圖書館」，把已發表 GWAS 找到的 SNP–性狀關聯整理起來，任何人都能查。

The GWAS Catalog is a public "library" of SNP–trait associations from published GWAS that anyone can search.

進階Advanced

由 NHGRI 與 EMBL-EBI 維護，提供人工整理的關聯、summary statistics 與標準化性狀註解，並連結 PGS Catalog。研究者用它取得 PRS 權重來源或比較前人結果。

Maintained by NHGRI and EMBL-EBI, it provides curated associations, summary statistics and standardized trait annotation, linked to the PGS Catalog. Researchers use it to source PRS weights or compare prior findings.

例子Examples

  • 查詢某性狀，即可看到相關 SNP、效應量、P 值與研究族群。Search a trait to see associated SNPs, effect sizes, p-values and study populations.
