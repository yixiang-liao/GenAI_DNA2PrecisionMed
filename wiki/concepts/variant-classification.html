變異致病性分類Variant Classification (ACMG/AMP)

Concept ID: variant-classification · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

找到變異後，要判斷它是「會致病」、「不會致病」，還是「還不知道」。臨床上用五個等級來表示。

After a variant is found, labs judge whether it causes disease, is harmless, or is still unknown, using five standard levels.

進階Advanced

ACMG/AMP（2015）五級：Pathogenic、Likely pathogenic、Uncertain significance（VUS）、Likely benign、Benign，依族群頻率、計算預測、功能與共分離等證據組合判定；適用於孟德爾疾病基因，不適用於 GWAS 的常見風險變異。

ACMG/AMP (2015) five tiers — Pathogenic, Likely pathogenic, Uncertain significance (VUS), Likely benign, Benign — combining population, computational, functional and segregation evidence; designed for Mendelian disease genes, not common GWAS risk variants.

例子Examples

  • 證據不足的變異標為 VUS，不應作為臨床決策依據。A variant with insufficient evidence is a VUS and should not drive clinical decisions.
