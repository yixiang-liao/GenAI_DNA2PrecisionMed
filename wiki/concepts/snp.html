單核苷酸多態性Single Nucleotide Polymorphism (SNP)

Concept ID: snp · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

SNP 是說明書中只差「一個字母」的地方。大部分 SNP 不影響健康，但有些會和疾病、藥物反應或身高等性狀有關。

A SNP is a place where the instruction book differs by a single letter. Most SNPs do not affect health, but some are linked to disease, drug response or traits like height.

進階Advanced

SNP 是單一鹼基位置的變異，常以 rsID（dbSNP 編號）表示。GWAS 主要以 SNP 為檢定單位；由於 LD，被檢定的 SNP 常只是附近因果變異的標記（tag）。

A SNP is a variant at a single base position, usually named by its dbSNP rsID. GWAS test mainly SNPs; because of LD, a tested SNP is often a tag for a nearby causal variant.

例子Examples

  • 某 SNP 在多數人是 C，少數人是 T。At a given SNP most people carry C and a minority carry T.
