孟德爾隨機化Mendelian Randomization (MR)

Concept ID: mendelian-randomization · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

出生時拿到哪個 allele 像抽籤，跟生活習慣無關。所以可以用「天生帶有提高某指標的變異」當作天然實驗，推論該指標是否真的導致疾病。

Which alleles you inherit is like a lottery at conception, unrelated to lifestyle. So variants that raise a measure can serve as a natural experiment to ask whether that measure causes disease.

進階Advanced L2

MR 以遺傳變異作為工具變數（instrumental variable），代表某暴露（如血脂），可利用 GWAS 摘要統計評估暴露與結果間的因果關係。三個強假設：變異與暴露相關、與任何混雜變數無關、只透過暴露影響結果；水平多效性會違反第三個假設。

MR uses genetic variants as instrumental variables for an exposure (e.g. blood lipids) and can assess causal relations from GWAS summary statistics. Three strong assumptions: the variant is associated with the exposure, not with any confounder, and with the outcome only through the exposure; horizontal pleiotropy breaks the third.

例子Examples

  • （示意）以提高 LDL 的變異為工具，估計 LDL 對冠心病的因果效應。(Illustrative) Using LDL-raising variants as instruments to estimate the causal effect of LDL on coronary disease.
