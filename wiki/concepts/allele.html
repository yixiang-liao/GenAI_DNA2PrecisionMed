對偶基因Allele

Concept ID: allele · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

同一個地址上可能有不同「版本」的字，例如有人是 A、有人是 G，這些版本就叫 allele。每人在每個位置有兩份：一份來自爸爸、一份來自媽媽。

The same address can hold different "versions" — some people have A, others G. Those versions are alleles. Everyone has two copies at each position: one from each parent.

進階Advanced

Allele 是某基因體位置上的序列版本，可以是單一鹼基或一段序列。兩份相同為 homozygous，不同為 heterozygous。在 GWAS/PRS 中需明確區分 reference allele、alternate allele 與 effect allele。

An allele is a sequence version at a genomic position (single base or segment). Two identical copies = homozygous; different = heterozygous. GWAS/PRS work must distinguish reference, alternate and effect alleles.

例子Examples

  • 某位置基因型為 A/G：此人帶有一個 A allele 和一個 G allele（heterozygous）。Genotype A/G at a site: one A allele and one G allele (heterozygous).
