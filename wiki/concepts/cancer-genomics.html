癌症基因體學Cancer Genomics

Concept ID: cancer-genomics · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

癌症是細胞的 DNA 出現後天變化而失控生長。分析腫瘤的突變、RNA 與其他分子資料，可以了解癌症類型並尋找治療方向。

Cancer arises when acquired DNA changes make cells grow out of control. Analysing a tumour's mutations, RNA and other molecules helps classify it and find treatment options.

進階Advanced

TCGA 在 DNA、RNA、蛋白質與表觀遺傳層次分析大量腫瘤；Pan-Cancer 比較 12 種腫瘤類型的共通性與差異。癌症基因體以 somatic 變異為主，需配對正常組織區分 germline 背景。

TCGA profiled many tumours at DNA, RNA, protein and epigenetic levels; Pan-Cancer compared commonalities and differences across 12 tumour types. Cancer genomics focuses on somatic variants, using matched normal tissue to separate germline background.

例子Examples

  • 腫瘤–正常配對定序：只在腫瘤出現的變異視為 somatic。Tumour–normal paired sequencing: variants present only in the tumour are somatic.
