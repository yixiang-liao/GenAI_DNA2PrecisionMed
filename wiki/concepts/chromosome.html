染色體Chromosome

Concept ID: chromosome · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

整套 DNA 很長，所以被分成好幾「冊」包裝起來，每一冊就是一條染色體。人類細胞裡有 23 對、共 46 條。

The whole DNA is very long, so it is packed into several "volumes" — each volume is a chromosome. Human cells have 23 pairs, 46 in total.

進階Advanced

每條染色體由一條連續的 DNA 分子與蛋白質組成。人類有 22 對體染色體（autosome）與一對性染色體（XX 或 XY）。基因體座標通常寫成「染色體:位置」，例如 chr1:12345。

Each chromosome is one continuous DNA molecule packaged with proteins. Humans have 22 autosome pairs and one sex-chromosome pair (XX or XY). Genomic coordinates are written as chromosome:position, e.g. chr1:12345.

例子Examples

  • 同一對染色體中，一條來自母親、一條來自父親。In each chromosome pair, one copy comes from the mother and one from the father.
