效應量（Beta / Odds Ratio）Effect Size (Beta / Odds Ratio) (β / OR)

Concept ID: effect-size · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

效應量回答「每多一個風險版本，影響有多大」。量身高時用 β（多幾公分）；看是否得病時用 Odds Ratio（勝算多幾倍）。

Effect size answers "how much does each extra risk copy change things". For height it is β (extra cm); for disease it is the odds ratio (how many times the odds).

進階Advanced

連續性狀：β 為每增加一個 effect allele 的性狀平均變化。二元性狀：OR = e^β，β 為 logistic 迴歸係數，即 log(OR)。β 的正負號取決於所選的 effect allele；交換 effect/other allele 時 β 變號、OR 變倒數。

Quantitative traits: β is the mean change per additional effect allele. Binary traits: OR = e^β where β is the logistic coefficient, i.e. log(OR). The sign of β depends on which allele is the effect allele; swapping alleles flips β's sign and inverts OR.

例子Examples

  • OR = 1.10 → β = ln(1.10) ≈ 0.095；換成另一個 allele 時 OR ≈ 0.91。OR = 1.10 → β = ln(1.10) ≈ 0.095; for the other allele OR ≈ 0.91.
