多效性Pleiotropy

Concept ID: pleiotropy · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

同一個基因變異可能同時影響好幾種性狀，就像一個開關同時控制客廳和走廊的燈。

One genetic variant can affect several traits — like one switch controlling the living-room and hallway lights.

進階Advanced L2

多方證據顯示複雜性狀普遍存在多效性；分析數千性狀的研究發現多數性狀相關基因座與不止一個性狀相關。遺傳相關可來自垂直多效性（A 導致 B）、水平多效性（變異直接影響兩性狀）、LD 誘發或多基因性誘發的多效性。水平多效性正是 Mendelian randomization「只透過暴露影響結果」假設要排除的情況。

Evidence points to widespread pleiotropy in complex traits; a study of thousands of traits found most trait-associated loci linked to more than one trait. Genetic correlation can come from vertical (A causes B), horizontal (variant affects both), LD-induced or polygenicity-induced pleiotropy. Horizontal pleiotropy is exactly what Mendelian randomization's 'only through the exposure' assumption must rule out.

例子Examples

  • （示意）同一基因座同時與血脂與冠心病相關。(Illustrative) One locus associated with both blood lipids and coronary artery disease.
