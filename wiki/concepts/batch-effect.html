批次效應Batch Effect

Concept ID: batch-effect · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

如果病人的樣本都在星期一處理、健康人的都在星期五處理，看到的差異可能只是「哪一天做的」，不是生物差異。這就是批次效應。

If all patient samples are processed on Monday and all controls on Friday, differences may reflect the day, not biology. That is a batch effect.

進階Advanced L2

批次效應是技術因素（建庫批次、定序 lane、日期）造成的系統性差異。設計上應把樣本隨機分配到批次與 lane，避免技術因素與實驗因素混淆；標準化後仍可能殘留，可用 ComBat、ARSyN 等方法校正。

Batch effects are systematic differences from technical factors (library batch, lane, date). Randomize samples across batches and lanes so technical and experimental factors are not confounded; residual effects after normalization can be corrected with ComBat or ARSyN.

例子Examples

  • （示意）PCA 圖上樣本依「處理日期」而不是「病例/對照」分成兩群。(Illustrative) On a PCA plot, samples cluster by processing date instead of case/control.
