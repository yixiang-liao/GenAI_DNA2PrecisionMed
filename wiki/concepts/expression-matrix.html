基因表現矩陣Gene Expression Matrix

Concept ID: expression-matrix · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

把結果整理成一張大表：每一列是一個基因、每一欄是一個樣本，格子裡是讀段數或表現量。

Results are arranged in a big table: one row per gene, one column per sample, with counts or expression values in the cells.

進階Advanced

原始計數矩陣（genes × samples，非負整數）是 DESeq2 等差異分析的輸入；TPM 矩陣適合視覺化與樣本內比較。矩陣需搭配樣本資訊表（條件、批次）才能建模，並處理批次效應。

A raw count matrix (genes × samples, non-negative integers) is the input to DESeq2-style differential analysis; TPM matrices suit visualization and within-sample comparison. A sample sheet (condition, batch) is required for modelling and batch correction.

例子Examples

  • 20,000 個基因 × 12 個樣本 = 240,000 個數值。20,000 genes × 12 samples = 240,000 values.
