變異偵測Variant Calling

Concept ID: variant-calling · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

把讀到的片段對回參考基因體後，電腦逐一檢查每個位置，判斷這個人是否帶有不同的版本，這就是 variant calling。

After reads are aligned to the reference, software checks each position to decide whether the person carries a different version — that is variant calling.

進階Advanced

典型步驟：比對（alignment）→ 去除重複 → 鹼基品質校正 → 統計模型判定 genotype → 過濾。GATK 提供這類工具框架並用於 1000 Genomes 與 TCGA。Germline 與 somatic calling 的統計假設不同，後者需處理腫瘤純度與次克隆。

Typical steps: alignment → duplicate marking → base-quality recalibration → statistical genotype calling → filtering. GATK provides such a framework and was used in 1000 Genomes and TCGA. Germline and somatic calling differ: somatic calling must handle tumour purity and subclones.

例子Examples

  • 某位置 30 條讀段中 15 條是 A、15 條是 G：可能是 heterozygous A/G。15 of 30 reads show A and 15 show G at a site: likely heterozygous A/G.
