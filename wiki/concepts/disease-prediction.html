疾病風險預測Disease Prediction

Concept ID: disease-prediction · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

預測是「估計機率」，不是「判決」。高風險的人不一定會生病，低風險的人也可能生病。預測結果要交由醫師結合其他資訊判讀。

Prediction estimates probability; it is not a verdict. High-risk people may stay well and low-risk people may get sick. Results should be interpreted by clinicians with other information.

進階Advanced

Khera 2018 主張可考慮將 PRS 納入臨床（冠狀動脈疾病高 PRS 族群的盛行率為同等風險單基因突變攜帶者的 20 倍），但這是英國血統單一世代的結果；Martin 2019 則警告跨族群準確度不一。PRS 引導篩檢是否改善臨床結果，目前本系統尚無可驗證證據（Pending）。

Khera 2018 proposed considering PRS in clinical care (high-PRS prevalence 20× that of monogenic carriers with comparable CAD risk), but in a single British-ancestry cohort; Martin 2019 warns of uneven accuracy across ancestries. Whether PRS-guided screening improves outcomes has no verified evidence in this system (Pending).

例子Examples

  • 研究會評估「把 PRS 加進年齡、血壓、膽固醇等臨床因子」是否改善預測；本站沒有已驗證的來源說明改善幅度。Studies evaluate whether adding a PRS to clinical factors such as age, blood pressure and cholesterol improves prediction; this site has no verified source on the size of that gain.
