藥物基因體學Pharmacogenomics (PGx)

Concept ID: pharmacogenomics · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

同一種藥，有人很有效、有人沒效、有人出現副作用，部分原因在基因。藥物基因體學希望做到「對的病人、對的藥、對的劑量」。

The same drug works well for some, not for others, and causes side effects in a few — partly because of genes. Pharmacogenomics aims for the right drug at the right dose for the right patient.

進階Advanced L2

藥物基因體學研究遺傳差異如何影響藥物反應與劑量需求。精準醫療計畫指出，暴露於多種療法的大型研究世代可望提供早期線索。注意：ACMG/AMP 序列變異解讀指引明言不適用於藥物基因體變異。

Pharmacogenomics studies how genetic differences shape drug response and dose. The Precision Medicine Initiative noted that large cohorts exposed to many therapies may provide early insights. Note that the ACMG/AMP sequence-variant guideline states it is not intended for pharmacogenomic variants.

例子Examples

  • （示意）某代謝酵素活性低的人，標準劑量下血中藥物濃度可能過高。(Illustrative) A person with a low-activity drug-metabolizing enzyme may reach excessive drug levels at a standard dose.
