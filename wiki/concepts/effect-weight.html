效應權重Effect Weight (β)

Concept ID: effect-weight · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner L1

每個 SNP 的影響大小不同，權重就是「這一票值幾分」，通常直接取自 GWAS 的效應量。

Each SNP matters by a different amount; the weight is how much its vote counts, usually taken from the GWAS effect size.

進階Advanced L2

權重來自 GWAS summary statistics 的 β 或 log(OR)（或 Z 分數）。C+T 直接使用邊際 β；LDpred、lassosum 等方法以 LD 與先驗分布（或 LASSO 型懲罰）收縮 summary statistics 的權重；以個體層級基因型聯合擬合的 BLUP、LASSO 預測則是另一類方法。權重應取自與目標樣本不重疊的 GWAS。

Weights come from GWAS summary statistics: β or log(OR) (or Z-scores). C+T uses marginal β directly; LDpred, lassosum and similar methods shrink summary-statistic weights using LD and priors (or LASSO-type penalties); BLUP or LASSO models fitted jointly on individual-level genotypes are a separate class. Weights must come from a GWAS that does not overlap the target sample.

例子Examples

  • OR = 1.2 → 權重 β = ln 1.2 ≈ 0.182。OR = 1.2 → weight β = ln 1.2 ≈ 0.182.
