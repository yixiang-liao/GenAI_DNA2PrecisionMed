基因表現Gene Expression

Concept ID: gene-expression · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

每個細胞都有同一本說明書，但肝細胞和腦細胞「翻開來用」的章節不同。一個基因被讀取（轉錄成 RNA）的多寡，就是它的表現量。

Every cell has the same book, but liver and brain cells open different chapters. How much a gene is read (transcribed into RNA) is its expression level.

進階Advanced

基因表現通常以 mRNA 豐度近似，會隨組織、時間與狀態改變。讀段數受轉錄本長度、定序深度與偏差影響，因此跨樣本比較前需要正規化。表現量改變不等於 DNA 突變：兩者分屬不同分子層次。

Gene expression is usually approximated by mRNA abundance, which varies by tissue, time and state. Read counts depend on transcript length, depth and biases, so normalization precedes cross-sample comparison. Expression change is not DNA mutation: they are different molecular layers.

例子Examples

  • 同一人的肝臟與腦組織，DNA 相同但表現譜不同。A person's liver and brain share DNA but have different expression profiles.
