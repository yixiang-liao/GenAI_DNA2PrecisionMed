連鎖不平衡Linkage Disequilibrium (LD)

Concept ID: ld · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

距離很近的 SNP 常常「一起被遺傳」，像同一節車廂的乘客一起上下車。所以看到其中一個，就能猜到另一個。

SNPs close together are often inherited together, like passengers in the same carriage. Seeing one lets you guess the other.

進階Advanced

LD 是不同基因座 allele 的非隨機關聯。常用 D = p_AB − p_A·p_B 與 r² = D² / [p_A(1−p_A)p_B(1−p_B)]。重組會隨世代打散 LD，且 LD 結構因族群歷史而異；這也是 GWAS 結果與 PRS 跨族群移植性受限的原因之一。

LD is the nonrandom association of alleles at different loci, measured by D = p_AB − p_A·p_B and r² = D² / [p_A(1−p_A)p_B(1−p_B)]. Recombination erodes LD over generations, and LD patterns differ by population history — one reason GWAS hits and PRS transfer imperfectly across ancestries.

例子Examples

  • r² = 1：兩個 SNP 完全相關，檢定任一個的結果相同。r² = 1: two SNPs are perfectly correlated; testing either gives the same result.
