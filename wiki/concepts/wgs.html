全基因體定序Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS)

Concept ID: wgs · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

WGS 是把整本說明書從頭讀到尾，包括會做蛋白質的章節和其他所有部分。

WGS reads the whole instruction book cover to cover — the protein-coding chapters and everything else.

進階Advanced

WGS 覆蓋編碼與非編碼區，可偵測 SNV、indel 與結構變異；覆蓋深度（coverage）影響偵測靈敏度。WGS、WES 與基因分型晶片常互補使用，例如 1000 Genomes Project 同時使用低覆蓋 WGS、深度 WES 與 array。

WGS covers coding and non-coding regions and detects SNVs, indels and structural variants; coverage depth affects sensitivity. WGS, WES and genotyping arrays are often combined — the 1000 Genomes Project used low-coverage WGS, deep WES and arrays together.

例子Examples

  • 30× 覆蓋度表示每個鹼基平均被讀到約 30 次。30× coverage means each base is read about 30 times on average.
