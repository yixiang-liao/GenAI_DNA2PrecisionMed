遺傳風險：相對與絕對Genetic Risk: Relative vs Absolute

Concept ID: genetic-risk · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner L1

「風險高三倍」聽起來很可怕，但如果原本機率是 1%，三倍也只是 3%。相對風險不等於絕對風險；而且基因之外，環境與生活也很重要。

"Three times the risk" sounds scary, but if the baseline is 1%, three times is 3%. Relative risk is not absolute risk — and environment and lifestyle matter beyond genes.

進階Advanced L2

PRS 研究常以「前 x% 與其餘人口比較」的勝算比（OR）或相對風險呈現。絕對風險 = 比較組的風險 × 相對風險；若研究報告的是 OR，只有在結果罕見時 OR 才近似相對風險。估計絕對風險需要目標族群的年齡、性別別發生率。Khera 等人指出，未來需考慮如何同時評估並溝通絕對與相對風險。

PRS studies often present an odds ratio or relative risk for the top x% versus the rest. Absolute risk = risk in the comparison group × relative risk; when a study reports an OR, OR ≈ RR only for rare outcomes. Estimating absolute risk needs age- and sex-specific incidence in the target population. Khera et al. note it will be important to consider how to assess and communicate both absolute and relative risks.

例子Examples

  • （模擬）比較組 10 年風險 2%、相對風險 1.5 → 絕對風險 2% × 1.5 = 3%。(Simulated) Comparison-group 10-year risk 2%, relative risk 1.5 → absolute risk 2% × 1.5 = 3%.
