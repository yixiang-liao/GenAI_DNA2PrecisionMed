遺傳風險：相對與絕對Genetic Risk: Relative vs Absolute

Concept ID: genetic-risk · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

「風險高三倍」聽起來很可怕，但如果原本機率是 1%，三倍也只是 3%。相對風險不等於絕對風險；而且基因之外，環境與生活也很重要。

"Three times the risk" sounds scary, but if the baseline is 1%, three times is 3%. Relative risk is not absolute risk — and environment and lifestyle matter beyond genes.

進階Advanced

PRS 研究多以相對風險（如前 x% 與其餘人口比較的 OR）報告。絕對風險 = 基準發生率 × 相對風險（在罕見結果下近似），需要目標族群的年齡、性別別發生率。Khera 等人也指出臨床需同時評估並溝通兩者。

PRS studies usually report relative risk (e.g. OR of top x% vs the rest). Absolute risk ≈ baseline incidence × relative risk (for rare outcomes) and requires age- and sex-specific incidence in the target population. Khera et al. note both must be assessed and communicated clinically.

例子Examples

  • （模擬）基準 10 年風險 2%、相對風險 1.5 → 絕對風險約 3%。(Simulated) Baseline 10-year risk 2%, relative risk 1.5 → absolute risk ≈ 3%.
