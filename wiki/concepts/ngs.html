次世代定序Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)

Concept ID: ngs · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

NGS 就像把一本書剪成數百萬張小紙條，同時快速讀完，再用電腦拼回原書。它讓定序變得更快、更便宜。

NGS is like cutting a book into millions of slips, reading them all at once, then reassembling the book by computer. It made sequencing faster and cheaper.

進階Advanced

NGS 泛指大規模平行定序。短讀段平台（數十至數百 bp）通量高、成本低；長讀段平台能跨越重複序列與結構變異，但（2016 年時）成本高、通量低。選擇平台取決於研究問題，而非一種技術取代全部。

NGS denotes massively parallel sequencing. Short-read platforms (tens to hundreds of bp) give high throughput at low cost; long-read platforms span repeats and structural variants but were (as of 2016) costlier and lower-throughput. Platform choice follows the research question.

例子Examples

  • 要找結構變異時，研究者可能選長讀段；大規模族群研究常選短讀段。To find structural variants a study may choose long reads; large population studies often choose short reads.
