生物標記Biomarker

Concept ID: biomarker · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

生物標記是可以測量、能反映身體狀態的指標，例如某個突變或某個基因的表現量，可能幫助判斷疾病類型或治療選擇。

A biomarker is a measurable indicator of body state — such as a mutation or a gene's expression level — that may help classify disease or choose treatment.

進階Advanced

分子生物標記可為 DNA（突變）、RNA（表現特徵）、蛋白或代謝物。從研究發現到臨床使用需經分析效度、臨床效度與臨床效用驗證（一般框架，本站未引用來源）。TCGA Pan-Cancer 的目標之一是依基因體特徵跨癌種延伸療法，這屬研究目標，非已證實結果。

Molecular biomarkers can be DNA (mutations), RNA (expression signatures), proteins or metabolites. Moving from discovery to clinic requires analytical validity, clinical validity and clinical utility (a general framework, not sourced on this site). Extending therapies across cancers by genomic profile is a TCGA Pan-Cancer aim, not a demonstrated result.

例子Examples

  • （示意）某腫瘤帶有特定突變時，可能適用針對該突變的標靶藥物。(Illustrative) A tumour with a specific mutation may be eligible for a drug targeting it.
