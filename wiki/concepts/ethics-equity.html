倫理、隱私與公平性Ethics, Privacy & Equity

Concept ID: ethics-equity · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

基因資料可以辨識個人、也牽涉家人，必須保護隱私。若研究只包含少數族群，工具可能對其他人比較不準，造成不公平。

Genetic data can identify a person and implicates relatives, so privacy must be protected. If research includes only some groups, tools may be less accurate for others, creating inequity.

進階Advanced

Martin 2019 主張公平的 PRS 需要提升研究族群多樣性並公開 summary statistics；Popejoy 2016 記錄 GWAS 族群組成偏差。其他議題包括知情同意、資料再識別風險、結果回饋與歧視風險。

Martin 2019 argues equitable PRS requires diverse cohorts and public summary statistics; Popejoy 2016 documents the skewed ancestry of GWAS. Other issues include informed consent, re-identification risk, return of results and discrimination.

例子Examples

  • 本專案只使用模擬基因型，不收集任何個人資料。This project uses only simulated genotypes and collects no personal data.
