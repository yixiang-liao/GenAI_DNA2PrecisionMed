資料整合Data Integration

Concept ID: data-integration · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

把 DNA 變異和 RNA 表現量放在一起看：例如某個變異是不是讓附近基因的表現變高或變低。

Put DNA variants and RNA expression side by side: for example, does a variant make a nearby gene's expression go up or down?

進階Advanced

整合策略可分為 genome first（從 GWAS locus 出發找機制）、phenotype first 與 environment first。典型例子是 eQTL mapping：以 genotype 預測表現量 (expression ~ dosage)。挑戰包括樣本對應、批次效應、不同資料尺度與缺失值。

Strategies include genome-first (from a GWAS locus toward mechanism), phenotype-first and environment-first. A typical example is eQTL mapping: expression ~ dosage. Challenges include sample matching, batch effects, differing data scales and missing values.

例子Examples

  • （示意）帶 G/G 的人某基因表現量平均高於 A/A：該 SNP 可能是 eQTL。(Illustrative) G/G carriers express a gene more than A/A carriers on average: the SNP may be an eQTL.

證據Evidence References

  • C-MO-02 共識Consensus

    多體學整合可從不同起點出發：genome first 從 GWAS 位點開始探究其致病機制，phenotype first 從其他層次出發，另有 environment first；並非只有一條固定路徑。Multi-omics integration can start from different points: genome-first starts from an associated locus to find mechanisms, phenotype-first starts from another layer, and there is also environment-first — there is no single fixed path.

    • S21 · Main text, section "Approaches to integrative analysis of multiple omics data"; Fig. 1 legend · E051
  • C-MO-07 共識Consensus

    eQTL mapping 將基因型與基因表現量連結，是 RNA-seq 分析的步驟之一，也是 Mutation/變異資料與 RNA Expression 整合的典型例子。eQTL mapping links genotype to gene expression; it is one of the RNA-seq analysis steps and a typical example of integrating variant data with RNA expression.

    • S09 · Main text, section "Integration with other data types" > "DNA sequencing" (eQTL mapping also listed as a step in the Abstract) · E056
