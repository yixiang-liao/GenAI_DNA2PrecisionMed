SNP 遺傳率SNP-Based Heritability (h²_SNP)

Concept ID: snp-heritability · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

一個性狀的個體差異裡，有多少能用所有 SNP 加起來解釋？這個比例就是 SNP 遺傳率，也是 PRS 預測能力的天花板。

How much of the person-to-person variation in a trait can all SNPs together explain? That share is SNP heritability — the ceiling for PRS prediction.

進階Advanced L2

SNP 遺傳率是所有（常見）SNP 能解釋的表現型變異比例，決定多基因分數的最大預測準確度；實際 PRS 表現還取決於多基因性與因果效應大小，以及 GWAS 樣本數。它也提供性狀遺傳架構的資訊，並引導研究設計（例如擴大樣本數）。

SNP heritability is the share of phenotypic variance explained by all (common) SNPs and sets the maximum predictive accuracy of polygenic scores; realised PRS accuracy also depends on polygenicity, effect sizes and GWAS sample size. It informs genetic architecture and study design.

例子Examples

  • （示意）h²_SNP = 0.25 → 即使 GWAS 無限大，PRS 最多解釋約 25% 的變異。(Illustrative) h²_SNP = 0.25 → even an infinitely large GWAS lets a PRS explain at most ~25% of variance.
