P 值與全基因體顯著性P-value & Genome-wide Significance

Concept ID: p-value · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

P 值表示「如果其實沒有關聯，看到這麼強結果的機率」。GWAS 一次檢查上百萬個 SNP，很容易碰巧中獎，所以門檻非常嚴格：p < 0.00000005。

A p-value is the chance of seeing a result this strong if there were truly no association. GWAS test millions of SNPs, so lucky hits are common — hence the strict threshold p < 0.00000005.

進階Advanced

全基因體顯著閾值 5 × 10⁻⁸ 大致相當於對約一百萬個獨立檢定做 Bonferroni 校正（0.05 / 10⁶）。P 值小只代表統計證據強，不代表效應大，也不代表因果；效應量要另外看 β 或 OR。

The 5 × 10⁻⁸ genome-wide threshold roughly corresponds to a Bonferroni correction for ~one million independent tests (0.05 / 10⁶). A small p-value means strong statistical evidence, not a large effect and not causation; look at β or OR for magnitude.

例子Examples

  • p = 3 × 10⁻⁹ 達全基因體顯著；p = 2 × 10⁻⁶ 則只是「提示性」關聯。p = 3 × 10⁻⁹ is genome-wide significant; p = 2 × 10⁻⁶ is only suggestive.
