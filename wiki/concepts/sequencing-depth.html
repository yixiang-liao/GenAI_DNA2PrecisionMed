定序深度 / 覆蓋度Sequencing Depth & Coverage

Concept ID: sequencing-depth · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

同一段 DNA 被讀到越多次，結果越可靠。30× 的意思是每個位置平均被讀了 30 次，就像同一句話請 30 個人聽寫再對答案。

The more times a stretch of DNA is read, the more reliable the result. 30× means each position was read about 30 times on average — like 30 people transcribing the same sentence.

進階Advanced L2

Coverage = 讀段總長 / 基因體長度。人類 WGS 常以約 30× 進行；隨機分布的定序錯誤（如 PacBio 長讀段）可靠足夠高的覆蓋度克服。在 RNA-seq 中，深度（library size）是每個樣本的讀段總數，需依實驗目的與重複數一起規劃，也是 TPM 等標準化要校正的因素。

Coverage = total read length / genome length. Human WGS is commonly run at ~30×; randomly distributed errors (e.g. PacBio long reads) can be overcome by high coverage. In RNA-seq, depth (library size) is the read count per sample, planned together with the number of replicates and corrected for by normalizations such as TPM.

例子Examples

  • （示意）3 Gb 基因體、9×10¹⁰ 個鹼基的讀段 → 約 30× 覆蓋。(Illustrative) 9×10¹⁰ sequenced bases over a 3 Gb genome → about 30× coverage.
