族群分層Population Stratification

Concept ID: population-stratification · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

如果病例組多半來自 A 族群、對照組多半來自 B 族群，任何在兩族群頻率不同的 SNP 都會「看起來」和疾病有關，其實只反映了血統差異。

If cases come mostly from population A and controls from B, any SNP whose frequency differs between A and B will look disease-associated — it only reflects ancestry.

進階Advanced L2

族群分層指樣本中存在遺傳上不同、且表現型平均值不同的亞族群，會產生假陽性/假陰性與偏誤統計量。GWAS 常先以主成分分析排除離群者，再把主成分當共變數放入迴歸；混合模型可進一步控制分層與親緣。

Population stratification is the presence of genetically distinct subpopulations with different mean phenotypes, producing false positives/negatives and biased statistics. GWAS first uses principal component analysis to exclude outliers, then includes components as regression covariates; mixed models further control stratification and relatedness.

例子Examples

  • （示意）乳糖耐受 SNP 在北歐頻率高；若病例多為北歐血統，此 SNP 可能與無關性狀出現假關聯。(Illustrative) A lactase-persistence SNP is common in Northern Europeans; if cases are mostly of that ancestry, it may show a spurious association with an unrelated trait.
