突變（生殖細胞系 / 體細胞）Mutation (Germline / Somatic)

Concept ID: mutation · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

從爸媽遺傳來、身體每個細胞都有的變異叫 germline；出生後才在部分細胞中出現的（例如癌細胞）叫 somatic，不會傳給孩子。

A variant inherited from parents and present in every cell is germline; one that arises later in some cells (e.g. cancer cells) is somatic and is not passed to children.

進階Advanced

Germline 變異存在於幾乎所有細胞，是 GWAS 與 PRS 的基礎；somatic 變異只存在部分細胞，是癌症基因體分析重點，常以腫瘤–正常配對樣本偵測。注意：Mutation 是 DNA 序列改變，基因表現（expression）是 RNA 量的改變，兩者不同。

Germline variants are in virtually every cell and underlie GWAS and PRS; somatic variants are present in some cells only, central to cancer genomics, often detected with tumour–normal pairs. Note: a mutation is a DNA sequence change, whereas gene expression is RNA abundance.

例子Examples

  • 腫瘤中出現、但同一人血液 DNA 中沒有的變異：somatic。A variant found in a tumour but not in the same person's blood DNA: somatic.
