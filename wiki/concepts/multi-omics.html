多體學Multi-Omics

Concept ID: multi-omics · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

只看 DNA 就像只看食譜，只看 RNA 像只看廚房正在煮什麼。多體學把食譜、正在煮的菜、端上桌的成品一起看，更容易了解問題出在哪裡。

Looking only at DNA is like reading the recipe; looking only at RNA is seeing what is cooking. Multi-omics looks at the recipe, the cooking and the served dish together to find where things go wrong.

進階Advanced

多體學整合基因體、轉錄體、表觀基因體、蛋白體、代謝體等層次，目標是追蹤疾病背後的資訊流動，而不是單一層次的關聯。它不是 WGS → RNA-seq → GWAS 的固定流程；研究可從任何層次出發。

Multi-omics integrates genome, transcriptome, epigenome, proteome, metabolome and other layers to follow the flow of information underlying disease, beyond single-layer associations. It is not a fixed WGS → RNA-seq → GWAS pipeline; studies may start from any layer.

例子Examples

  • 同一批腫瘤同時有突變、表現與甲基化資料，可交互比對。The same tumours with mutation, expression and methylation data can be cross-compared.
