C+T 方法Clumping + Thresholding (C+T)

Concept ID: clumping-thresholding · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

把 GWAS 結果做成 PRS 前，先把「一起遺傳、重複計算」的 SNP 只留一個（clumping），再只保留 P 值夠小的（thresholding）。

Before building a PRS, keep one SNP per group of co-inherited ones (clumping), then keep only those with small enough P values (thresholding).

進階Advanced L2

C+T 是標準 PRS 方法：依 LD 進行 clumping，再以多個 P 值門檻篩選 SNP，兩者都需要估計 LD。批評包括 LD 相關門檻常被任意選定，以及在多個 P 值門檻中挑最佳 PRS 會過度擬合目標資料，因此需獨立驗證樣本。另一類方法以 lasso 迴歸等收縮（shrinkage）處理效應量。

C+T is the standard PRS method: clump by LD, then filter SNPs at several P-value thresholds; both require LD estimates. Criticisms: the LD correlation threshold is often arbitrary, and picking the best of many thresholds overfits the target data, so an independent validation sample is needed. Other methods shrink effect sizes, e.g. with lasso regression.

例子Examples

  • （示意）r² < 0.1、窗口 250 kb clumping 後，於 P < 5×10⁻⁸、10⁻⁴、0.05 等門檻分別建分數。(Illustrative) Clump at r² < 0.1 within 250 kb, then build scores at P < 5×10⁻⁸, 10⁻⁴ and 0.05.
