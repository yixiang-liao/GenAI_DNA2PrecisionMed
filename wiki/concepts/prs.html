多基因風險分數Polygenic Risk Score (PRS)

Concept ID: prs · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

很多疾病不是單一基因造成，而是成千上萬個小影響累加。PRS 把一個人帶有的風險版本「加權加總」成一個分數，用來和其他人比較高低；它是相對位置，不是你一定會不會得病的答案。

Many diseases come from thousands of small effects added together. A PRS adds up a person's risk versions, weighted, into one score for comparison with others; it is a relative position, not a yes/no answer about getting the disease.

進階Advanced

PRS 估計個體的遺傳傾向（genetic liability），通常只解釋性狀變異的一小部分。其表現依賴 GWAS 樣本量、目標族群與 base 族群的相似度，以及 LD 處理方式。個人與臨床效用仍有爭議；目前證據主要支持群體層級的風險分層。

A PRS estimates genetic liability and typically explains a small fraction of trait variance. Performance depends on GWAS sample size, similarity between base and target populations, and LD handling. Personal and clinical utility is debated; evidence mainly supports group-level stratification.

例子Examples

  • （模擬）某人 PRS 落在第 90 百分位：表示在同一參考族群中高於 90% 的人，並非 90% 機率罹病。(Simulated) A PRS at the 90th percentile means higher than 90% of the reference population — not a 90% chance of disease.
