Wiki / 多基因風險分數Polygenic Risk Score

PRS 驗證PRS Validation

Concept ID: prs-validation · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

做好的分數要拿到「沒看過的新一群人」測試，看看高分的人是不是真的比較常得病。用同一群人自己考自己，會高估效果。

A score must be tested on new people it has never seen, to check that high scorers really get the disease more often. Testing on the same people overstates performance.

進階Advanced

常見指標：AUC、R²（liability scale）、高低百分位的 OR。base 與 target 樣本不可重疊，也應移除近親。Khera 2018 在 UK Biobank 以 Phase I 選分數、Phase II 獨立測試，但兩者皆為英國血統，結果不能直接外推到其他族群。

Common metrics: AUC, liability-scale R², OR between top and bottom percentiles. Base and target samples must not overlap and close relatives should be removed. Khera 2018 selected scores in UK Biobank Phase I and tested in Phase II, but both were British-ancestry, so results do not transfer directly to other ancestries.

例子Examples

  • AUC = 0.5 代表跟擲硬幣一樣；越接近 1 區分能力越好。AUC = 0.5 is coin-flip; closer to 1 means better discrimination.

證據Evidence References

  • C-PRS-06 單一研究Single Study Finding

    Khera 等人在 UK Biobank（英國血統參與者）建立並驗證五種疾病的全基因體 PRS，可找出 8.0%（冠狀動脈疾病）到 1.5%（乳癌）的人口，其勝算比（odds ratio）相較其餘人口 ≥3。Khera et al. developed and validated genome-wide PRS for five diseases in UK Biobank participants of British ancestry, identifying 8.0% (coronary artery disease) to 1.5% (breast cancer) of the population with an odds ratio ≥3 versus the remainder of the population.

    • S20 · Abstract; Main text (UK Biobank study population); Online Methods "Statistical analysis within the testing dataset" (see also Table 2, "Odds ratio ≥3.0") · E043
  • C-PRS-07 共識Consensus

    PRS 驗證時，base（GWAS）資料與 target 資料不能有樣本重疊，否則 PRS 與性狀的關聯會被嚴重高估。When validating a PRS, sample overlap between base (GWAS) and target data must be eliminated, because it can substantially inflate the PRS–trait association.

    • S16 · Section 2 "Quality Control of Base and Target data", subsection "Sample overlap" · E044
