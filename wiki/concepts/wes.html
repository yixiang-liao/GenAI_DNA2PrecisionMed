全外顯子定序Whole Exome Sequencing (WES)

Concept ID: wes · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

WES 只讀說明書中「真正寫製作方法」的段落（外顯子），大約只占全部的 1.5%，所以比較便宜。

WES reads only the paragraphs that actually describe how to make proteins (exons) — about 1.5% of the whole book — so it costs less.

進階Advanced

WES 先以探針捕捉外顯子再定序，適合尋找影響蛋白質的變異；但會漏掉多數非編碼調控變異，且捕捉效率不均使覆蓋度較不平均。

WES captures exons with probes before sequencing; it suits protein-altering variant discovery but misses most non-coding regulatory variants, and capture efficiency makes coverage less uniform.

例子Examples

  • 疑似單基因遺傳疾病的診斷可能使用 WES 或 WGS（一般實務，依指引而異；本站未引用來源）。Diagnosis of a suspected monogenic disorder may use WES or WGS (general practice that varies by guideline; not sourced on this site).
