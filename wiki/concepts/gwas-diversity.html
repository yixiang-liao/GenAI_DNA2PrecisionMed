研究族群多樣性Population Diversity in GWAS

Concept ID: gwas-diversity · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

過去大部分 GWAS 的參與者是歐洲血統。只用某一群人找到的結果，套用到其他族群時不一定準確。

Most past GWAS participants were of European ancestry. Results from one group may not hold as well in others.

進階Advanced

Popejoy & Fullerton（2016）：2009 年 96% 為歐洲血統，2016 年非歐洲約 20%，但非洲、拉丁美洲血統、西語裔與原住民族群合計 <4%。族群間 allele 頻率與 LD 結構不同，造成發現偏差與 PRS 移植性下降。

Popejoy & Fullerton (2016): 96% European in 2009; ~20% non-European by 2016, but African and Latin American ancestry, Hispanic and indigenous groups together <4%. Differences in allele frequencies and LD cause discovery bias and reduced PRS portability.

例子Examples

  • 某 SNP 在一族群常見、在另一族群罕見，其可檢測性就不同。A SNP common in one population but rare in another has different detectability.
