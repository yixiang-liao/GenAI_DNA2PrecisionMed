Wiki / 多基因風險分數Polygenic Risk Score

PRS 跨族群適用性PRS Portability Across Ancestries

Concept ID: prs-portability · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

如果分數主要用歐洲族群的資料做出來，用在亞洲或非洲族群時就常常沒那麼準。

If a score is built mostly from European data, it is often less accurate in Asian or African populations.

進階Advanced

Martin 等人（2019）指出目前 PRS 在歐洲血統中準確度高出數倍，源自 GWAS 的歐洲偏重；原因包括 allele 頻率差異、LD 結構差異與標記 SNP 不同。對策：多元族群 GWAS、跨族群方法，並公開 summary statistics。

Martin et al. (2019) showed current PRSs are several-fold more accurate in European ancestry because GWAS are Eurocentric; causes include allele-frequency and LD differences and differing tag SNPs. Remedies: diverse GWAS, multi-ancestry methods, public summary statistics.

例子Examples

  • 同一分數在兩族群的 R² 可能相差數倍。The same score's R² can differ several-fold between two populations.
