Wiki / 精準醫療Precision Medicine

臨床應用Clinical Application

Concept ID: clinical-application · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

研究上的發現要用在病人身上，需要合格實驗室檢測、專業人員判讀，以及清楚的風險溝通。這個網站是教材，不是診斷工具。

Using research findings for patients requires certified labs, expert interpretation and clear risk communication. This website is teaching material, not a diagnostic tool.

進階Advanced

ACMG 建議臨床分子遺傳檢測於 CLIA 認證實驗室進行，由具認證的專家判讀，並以五級分類描述孟德爾疾病變異。研究級 PRS 與臨床檢測之間仍需驗證效度、效用、族群適用性與倫理規範。

ACMG recommends clinical molecular genetic testing in CLIA-approved labs with board-certified interpretation and five-tier classification for Mendelian variants. Research-grade PRS still needs validity, utility, population applicability and ethical safeguards before clinical use.

例子Examples

  • 消費者基因檢測的結果，不能直接當作臨床診斷。Direct-to-consumer genetic results are not a clinical diagnosis.

證據Evidence References

    ACMG 強烈建議臨床分子遺傳檢測在 CLIA 認證實驗室進行，結果由具專科認證的臨床分子遺傳學家或同等專家判讀。ACMG strongly recommends that clinical molecular genetic testing be performed in a CLIA-approved laboratory, with results interpreted by a board-certified clinical molecular geneticist or equivalent.
