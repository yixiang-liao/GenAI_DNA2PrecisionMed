多基因風險分數Polygenic Risk Score · 5/10

PRS 計算PRS Calculation

Concept ID: prs-calculation · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner L1

計算方法很簡單：每個 SNP 的「權重 × 你有幾個」，全部加起來。PRS = Σ(βᵢ × Gᵢ)。

The calculation is simple: for each SNP, weight × how many copies you have, then add them up. PRS = Σ(βᵢ × Gᵢ).

進階Advanced L2

PRSⱼ = Σᵢ βᵢ · Gᵢⱼ。實務步驟：base GWAS 與 target 資料 QC → allele 對齊 → 處理 LD（clumping 或 LD-aware 收縮）→ 選擇 SNP 集合（P 值門檻或全基因體）→ 計算 → 在獨立樣本評估。分數常再標準化為 z-score 或百分位。

PRSⱼ = Σᵢ βᵢ · Gᵢⱼ. Practical steps: QC base GWAS and target data → harmonize alleles → handle LD (clumping or LD-aware shrinkage) → choose SNP set (P threshold or genome-wide) → compute → evaluate in an independent sample. Scores are often standardized to z-scores or percentiles.

  • C-PRS-03 共識Consensus

    常見的「標準方法」是 C+T（clumping + thresholding）：以 clumping 處理 LD，再依 P 值門檻篩選 SNP。另一類方法對 summary statistics 的權重做收縮（如 LDpred，或 lassosum 這類 LASSO 型懲罰）；以個體層級基因型聯合擬合的 BLUP、LASSO 預測模型則是不同的一類。The standard approach is C+T (clumping + thresholding): LD is handled by clumping and SNPs are selected by a P-value threshold. Other methods shrink summary-statistic weights (e.g. LDpred, or LASSO-type penalties as in lassosum); BLUP or LASSO models fitted jointly on individual-level genotypes are a separate class.

    • S16 · Section 3.2 "Controlling for Linkage Disequilibrium"; Section 3.1 "Shrinkage of GWAS effect size estimates"; Section 1 "Introduction to Polygenic Risk Scores" · E039
