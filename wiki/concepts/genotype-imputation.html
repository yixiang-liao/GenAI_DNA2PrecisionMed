基因型插補Genotype Imputation

Concept ID: genotype-imputation · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

晶片只測了一部分 SNP。因為附近的 SNP 常一起遺傳，我們可以參考已完整定序的人，把沒測到的 SNP「填空」推回來。

Arrays measure only some SNPs. Because nearby SNPs travel together, missing ones can be filled in by comparing with fully sequenced people.

進階Advanced L2

Imputation 以觀測 SNP 推得的 haplotype，對照完整定序的參考面板（如 1000 Genomes、TOPMed）預測未測變異；它能部分彌補觀測 SNP 與未觀測因果變異之間不完全的 LD。品質常以 info score 篩選（例如 > 0.8）。定相（phasing）——推估 allele 來自母方或父方——是其前置步驟。

Imputation predicts unmeasured variants from haplotypes inferred from observed SNPs, matched against a sequenced reference panel (e.g. 1000 Genomes, TOPMed), partly recovering information lost to imperfect LD with causal variants. Quality is filtered by info score (e.g. > 0.8). Phasing — estimating whether alleles came from the mother or father — precedes it.

例子Examples

  • （示意）晶片測了 60 萬個 SNP，插補後可分析數百萬個變異。(Illustrative) An array with 600,000 SNPs yields millions of analysable variants after imputation.
