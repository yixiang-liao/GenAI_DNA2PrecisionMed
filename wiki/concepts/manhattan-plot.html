曼哈頓圖Manhattan Plot

Concept ID: manhattan-plot · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

曼哈頓圖是一種呈現 GWAS 結果的圖；圖上的橫線就是全基因體顯著門檻 5 × 10⁻⁸。其座標定義的引用來源尚待驗證（Pending Verification）。

The Manhattan plot is a way to display GWAS results; the horizontal line marks the 5 × 10⁻⁸ genome-wide threshold. The cited definition of its axes is Pending Verification.

進階Advanced

本系統只發布已驗證部分：顯著閾值線對應 p = 5 × 10⁻⁸。座標軸定義主張（C-GW-09）目前因語料庫中缺少可驗證來源而標記為 Pending Verification，詳見 Evidence 頁。

Only the verified part is published: the threshold line corresponds to p = 5 × 10⁻⁸. The axis-definition claim (C-GW-09) is Pending Verification because no verifiable source is in the corpus; see the Evidence page.
