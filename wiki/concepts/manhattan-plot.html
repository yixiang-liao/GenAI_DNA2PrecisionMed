曼哈頓圖Manhattan Plot

Concept ID: manhattan-plot · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

曼哈頓圖是呈現 GWAS 結果的圖：每個點是一個 SNP，左右位置是它在染色體上的位置，越高代表關聯越強；圖上的橫線就是全基因體顯著門檻 5 × 10⁻⁸。因為一根根柱子像曼哈頓的天際線而得名。

A Manhattan plot displays GWAS results: each dot is a SNP, placed left to right by its position on the chromosomes and higher for stronger association; the horizontal line marks the 5 × 10⁻⁸ genome-wide threshold.

進階Advanced

x 軸：SNP 依基因體位置排列（依染色體分段）；y 軸：−log₁₀(P)，例如 P = 10⁻⁸ 對應 8。顯著閾值線對應 P = 5 × 10⁻⁸（−log₁₀ ≈ 7.3）。常與 Q–Q plot 一起檢查檢定統計是否膨脹（C-GW-09）。

x-axis: SNPs ordered by genomic position (grouped by chromosome); y-axis: −log10(P), e.g. P = 10⁻⁸ plots at 8. The threshold line is P = 5 × 10⁻⁸ (−log10 ≈ 7.3). It is usually read together with a Q–Q plot to check for inflated test statistics (C-GW-09).
