基因Gene

Concept ID: gene · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

基因是說明書裡的一個「章節」，通常負責製造一種蛋白質。人類大約有 2 萬個會做蛋白質的基因。

A gene is a "chapter" of the instruction book, usually describing how to make one protein. Humans have about 20,000 protein-coding genes.

進階Advanced

基因是遺傳的基本單位，包含外顯子、內含子與調控序列。多數基因經轉錄與轉譯產生蛋白質，也有基因只產生功能性 RNA。蛋白質編碼基因的外顯子合起來就是 exome。

The gene is the basic unit of inheritance, comprising exons, introns and regulatory sequence. Most genes are transcribed and translated into proteins; some yield only functional RNA. Exons of protein-coding genes together form the exome.

例子Examples

  • （示意）若某基因的外顯子發生改變，可能改變其蛋白質產物；位於基因之間的變異則可能影響調控。(Illustrative) A change in a gene's exon may alter its protein product, while a variant between genes may affect regulation.
