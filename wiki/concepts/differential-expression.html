差異基因表現分析Differential Gene Expression (DGE)

Concept ID: differential-expression · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

比較兩組（例如病人與健康者），找出哪些基因的表現量明顯不同。要有多個重複樣本，才能分辨真實差異與隨機波動。

Compare two groups (e.g. patients vs healthy) to find genes whose expression clearly differs. Replicates are needed to tell real differences from noise.

進階Advanced

DESeq2 以負二項分布建模計數：Kᵢⱼ ~ NB(μᵢⱼ, αᵢ)，log₂ μᵢⱼ 以實驗設計線性建模；對 dispersion αᵢ 與 log₂ fold change 做 shrinkage 以穩定估計，再以多重檢定校正（如 Benjamini–Hochberg FDR）。

DESeq2 models counts as Kᵢⱼ ~ NB(μᵢⱼ, αᵢ) with log₂ μᵢⱼ linear in the design; dispersions αᵢ and log₂ fold changes are shrunk for stability, followed by multiple-testing correction (e.g. Benjamini–Hochberg FDR).

例子Examples

  • log₂FC = 1 代表表現量約為對照組的 2 倍。log₂FC = 1 means roughly 2× the control expression.
