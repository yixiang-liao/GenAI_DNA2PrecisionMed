GWAS 與統計遺傳學GWAS & Statistical Genetics · 10/11

精細定位Fine-Mapping

Concept ID: fine-mapping · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

GWAS 找到一整節「車廂」的 SNP 都有訊號，fine-mapping 則是想找出到底是哪一位「乘客」真正造成影響。

GWAS flags a whole "carriage" of SNPs; fine-mapping tries to find which "passenger" actually causes the effect.

進階Advanced L2

Fine-mapping 試圖從 GWAS 訊號中找出一個或多個因果變異，單靠統計關聯受樣本數與 LD 限制：區分因果變異與其 LD 夥伴的證據與 n(1 − r²) 成正比，因此樣本數越大、r² 越小，越容易分開。

Fine-mapping seeks the causal variant(s) behind a GWAS signal, Statistics alone are limited by sample size and LD: the evidence separating a causal variant from an LD partner is proportional to n(1 − r²), so larger samples and lower r² make them easier to tell apart.

例子Examples

  • （示意）兩個 SNP r² = 0.9：n(1 − r²) 只有 0.1n，需要非常大的樣本才分得開。(Illustrative) Two SNPs with r² = 0.9: n(1 − r²) is only 0.1n, so a very large sample is needed to separate them.
