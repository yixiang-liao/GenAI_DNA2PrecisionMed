全基因體關聯研究Genome-Wide Association Study (GWAS)

Concept ID: gwas · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

GWAS 比較「有某疾病」和「沒有」的一大群人，在整個基因體裡找出在病人中比較常見的 SNP。找到的是「關聯」，不代表那個 SNP 就是病因：關聯不等於因果。

GWAS compares large groups with and without a disease, scanning the whole genome for SNPs that are more common in patients. What it finds is association — not proof that the SNP causes the disease.

進階Advanced

對每個 SNP 以迴歸檢定 genotype dosage 與 phenotype 的關聯（連續性狀用線性迴歸、二元性狀用邏輯斯迴歸），並納入主成分等共變數校正族群分層。顯著 SNP 常透過 LD 標記附近未知的因果變異，需要 fine-mapping 與功能研究才能推論機制。

Each SNP is tested by regressing phenotype on genotype dosage (linear for quantitative, logistic for binary traits), adjusting for covariates such as principal components to control population stratification. Significant SNPs often tag unknown causal variants via LD; fine-mapping and functional work are needed to infer mechanism.

例子Examples

  • （示意）10,000 名病例與 10,000 名對照中，某 SNP 的 T allele 頻率分別為 32% 與 28%。(Illustrative) Among 10,000 cases and 10,000 controls, a SNP's T allele frequency is 32% vs 28%.
