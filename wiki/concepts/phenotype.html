表現型Phenotype

Concept ID: phenotype · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

表現型是看得到或量得到的特徵，例如身高、血型或是否罹病。它受到基因和環境（飲食、運動、生活）一起影響。

A phenotype is a trait you can see or measure — height, blood type, or having a disease. It is shaped by both genes and environment (diet, exercise, lifestyle).

進階Advanced

Phenotype 可以是連續性狀（身高、LDL）或二元性狀（病例/對照），這會決定 GWAS 使用線性或邏輯斯迴歸，效應量也分別以 β 或 OR 表示。Genotype → Phenotype 不是決定論關係。

Phenotypes can be quantitative (height, LDL) or binary (case/control), which determines linear vs logistic regression in GWAS and β vs OR as the effect size. Genotype → phenotype is not deterministic.

例子Examples

  • 同卵雙胞胎基因型幾乎相同，但體重可能因生活型態而不同。Identical twins share nearly the same genotype but may differ in weight due to lifestyle.
