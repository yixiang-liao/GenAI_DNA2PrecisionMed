變異呼叫格式Variant Call Format (VCF)

Concept ID: vcf · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

VCF 是記錄「哪裡不一樣」的標準表格：哪條染色體、哪個位置、原本是什麼、變成什麼，以及每個人的基因型。

VCF is the standard table of "where it differs": chromosome, position, reference, alternate, and each person's genotype.

進階Advanced

VCF 為文字格式，含 header（##）與固定欄位 CHROM, POS, ID, REF, ALT, QUAL, FILTER, INFO, FORMAT 及樣本欄；可存 SNP、indel 與結構變異，常以 bgzip 壓縮並以 tabix 建索引。

VCF is a text format with header lines (##) and fixed columns CHROM, POS, ID, REF, ALT, QUAL, FILTER, INFO, FORMAT plus sample columns; it stores SNPs, indels and SVs and is usually bgzip-compressed and tabix-indexed.

例子Examples

  • （示意）chr1 1000 demo1 A G … GT 0|1 → 此人在該位置為 A/G(Illustrative) chr1 1000 demo1 A G … GT 0|1 → this person is A/G at that site
