基因體學Genomics

Concept ID: genomics · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

基因體學研究「整本說明書」，而不是一次只看一個基因。GWAS、WGS 都屬於基因體學工具。

Genomics studies the whole instruction book rather than one gene at a time. GWAS and WGS are genomics tools.

進階Advanced

基因體學涵蓋序列、變異與結構分析。基因分型晶片帶來大規模 GWAS，加上全轉錄本測量，形成 integrative genetics，是多體學的起點。基因體層次的資料多為一生大致固定的 germline 變異（癌症中的 somatic 變異除外）。

Genomics covers sequence, variation and structure. Genotyping arrays enabled large GWAS and, with transcriptome-wide measurement, gave rise to integrative genetics — the starting point of multi-omics. Genome-level data are mostly germline variants fixed over life (except somatic variants in cancer).

例子Examples

  • 用 WGS 比較一家人的變異以找出遺傳疾病原因。Using WGS to compare variants across a family to find a hereditary cause.
