去氧核糖核酸Deoxyribonucleic Acid (DNA)

Concept ID: dna · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

DNA 就像一本用四個字母（A、C、G、T）寫成的說明書，記錄生物如何長成、如何運作。兩股 DNA 像拉鍊一樣扣在一起：A 一定對 T，C 一定對 G。

DNA is like an instruction book written with four letters (A, C, G, T) describing how an organism is built and runs. Two strands zip together: A always pairs with T, and C with G.

進階Advanced

DNA 是由去氧核糖、磷酸骨架與四種含氮鹼基組成的雙股聚合物，兩股以互補鹼基配對（A–T、C–G）反向平行結合。互補性讓 DNA 能被複製，也讓定序與比對成為可能：讀到一股即可推得另一股。

DNA is a double-stranded polymer of a deoxyribose–phosphate backbone and four nitrogenous bases; the antiparallel strands are held by complementary pairing (A–T, C–G). Complementarity enables replication and underlies sequencing and alignment: reading one strand determines the other.

例子Examples

  • 序列 5'-ATGC-3' 的互補股是 3'-TACG-5'。The complement of 5'-ATGC-3' is 3'-TACG-5'.
