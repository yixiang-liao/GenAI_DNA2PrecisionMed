精準醫療Precision Medicine

Concept ID: precision-medicine · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

傳統醫療常以「平均的病人」設計治療；精準醫療則考慮每個人在基因、環境與生活型態的差異，找出更適合的預防與治療方式。

Traditional care is often designed for the "average patient"; precision medicine considers each person's genes, environment and lifestyle to choose better prevention and treatment.

進階Advanced

美國 Precision Medicine Initiative（2015）近期以癌症為重點，長期擴及所有疾病。基因資訊只是其中一部分，必須與臨床資料、環境暴露和生活型態整合；並非所有基因檢測結果都能改變臨床決策。

The US Precision Medicine Initiative (2015) focused near-term on cancer and long-term on all disease. Genetic information is one component, integrated with clinical data, environment and lifestyle; not every genetic result changes clinical decisions.

例子Examples

  • 依腫瘤的分子特徵選擇治療，而非只看腫瘤長在哪個器官。Choosing therapy by a tumour's molecular profile, not only by the organ it arose in.
