路徑分析Pathway Analysis

Concept ID: pathway-analysis · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

一次看到幾百個差異基因很難理解。路徑分析問的是：這些基因是不是集中在某些生物功能（例如發炎、細胞分裂）？

Hundreds of differential genes are hard to interpret. Pathway analysis asks whether they cluster in certain biological functions such as inflammation or cell division.

進階Advanced

方法包括過度表現分析（超幾何檢定）與 Gene Set Enrichment Analysis（依排序檢定基因集合是否集中於一端）。結果依賴所選基因集合資料庫與背景基因，需多重檢定校正。

Methods include over-representation analysis (hypergeometric test) and Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (testing whether a gene set concentrates at one end of a ranked list). Results depend on the gene-set database and background, and need multiple-testing correction.

例子Examples

  • （示意）差異基因中有大量細胞週期相關基因 → 提示細胞增生改變。(Illustrative) Many cell-cycle genes among DE genes → suggests altered proliferation.
