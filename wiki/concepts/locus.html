基因座Locus

Concept ID: locus · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

Locus 就是「地址」：告訴你在哪一條染色體的哪個位置。基因是內容，locus 是位置。

A locus is an "address": which chromosome and which position. A gene is content; a locus is a place.

進階Advanced

Locus 可以指單一鹼基位置，也可以指一段區域，例如一個基因或 GWAS 找到的關聯區段。GWAS 論文常說的「risk locus」是一個關聯區域，未必等於某一個基因。

A locus can be a single base position or a region such as a gene or a GWAS association interval. A "risk locus" in a GWAS paper is an associated region, not necessarily a single gene.

例子Examples

  • （示意）「chr1:1,000,000」是一個單鹼基 locus；「chr1:1,000,000–1,050,000」是一個區域 locus。(Illustrative) "chr1:1,000,000" is a single-base locus; "chr1:1,000,000–1,050,000" is a regional locus.
