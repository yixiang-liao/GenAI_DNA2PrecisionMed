轉錄體學Transcriptomics · 6/8

選擇性剪接Alternative Splicing & Isoforms

Concept ID: alternative-splicing · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

基因像一份有很多段落的食譜，做菜前會剪掉不需要的段落（intron）。不同的剪法可以從同一個基因做出不同版本的 RNA（isoform）。

A gene is like a recipe with many paragraphs; unneeded ones (introns) are cut out before cooking. Different cuts make different RNA versions (isoforms) from one gene.

進階Advanced L2

轉錄體學的目標之一是決定基因的轉錄結構（起始點、5′/3′ 端、剪接模式）。剪接移除 intron；RNA-seq 可藉 GT–AG 辨識 exon–exon junction。同一基因的 isoform 共用 exon，比對到轉錄體時 multi-mapping reads 增加；isoform 分析較適合 paired-end 讀段。

Determining transcript structure — start sites, 5′/3′ ends, splicing patterns — is a core aim of transcriptomics. Splicing removes introns; RNA-seq finds exon–exon junctions via GT–AG motifs. Isoforms share exons, raising multi-mapping when reads are aligned to the transcriptome; paired-end reads suit isoform analysis.

例子Examples

  • （示意）基因 X 的 isoform 1 含 exon 1-2-3，isoform 2 跳過 exon 2。(Illustrative) Isoform 1 of gene X uses exons 1-2-3; isoform 2 skips exon 2.

    轉錄體學的主要目標包括：盤點所有轉錄本、決定基因的轉錄結構（起始位置、5′/3′ 端、剪接模式等），以及量化各轉錄本在不同條件下的表現量。Key aims of transcriptomics are to catalogue all transcripts, to determine the transcriptional structure of genes — start sites, 5′ and 3′ ends, splicing patterns and other post-transcriptional modifications — and to quantify each transcript's expression under different conditions.

  • C-TX-10 共識Consensus

    剪接時 intron 被移除；RNA-seq 可藉由剪接位點兩側的 GT–AG 二核苷酸辨識 exon–exon junction，並以 intron 序列的低表現加以確認。Introns are removed during splicing; RNA-seq can identify exon–exon junctions by the GT–AG dinucleotides flanking splice sites and confirm them by the low expression of intronic sequence.

  • C-TX-11 共識Consensus

    同一基因的不同 isoform 共用部分 exon，因此讀段比對到轉錄體時會出現較多 multi-mapping reads；paired-end 讀段較適合 isoform 表現分析。Isoforms of the same gene share exons, so mapping reads to the transcriptome yields more multi-mapping reads; paired-end reads are preferable for isoform expression analysis.
