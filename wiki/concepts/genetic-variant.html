遺傳變異Genetic Variant

Concept ID: genetic-variant · Last Updated: 2026-11-13

初學者Beginner

任何和參考序列不一樣的地方都叫變異：可能是換一個字（SNP）、少幾個字或多幾個字（indel），或是一大段被搬動（結構變異）。

Any difference from the reference sequence is a variant: a swapped letter (SNP), a few letters missing or added (indel), or a large rearranged segment (structural variant).

進階Advanced

變異依大小與型態分為 SNV/SNP、indel 與 structural variant（含 CNV）。1000 Genomes Project 在 2,504 人中描述超過 8,800 萬個變異，說明人類變異極為豐富，且大多數是常見、非致病的。

Variants are classified by size and form: SNV/SNP, indel, and structural variants (including CNVs). The 1000 Genomes Project described >88 million variants in 2,504 people, showing that human variation is abundant and mostly common and benign.

例子Examples

  • 參考序列 ACGT，樣本序列 ACCT：第 3 個位置為 G→C 的 SNV。Reference ACGT, sample ACCT: a G→C SNV at position 3.
