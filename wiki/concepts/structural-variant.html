結構變異Structural Variant (SV)

Concept ID: structural-variant · Last Updated: 2026-11-23

初學者Beginner L1

除了單一字母的改變，DNA 也可能有一大段被刪掉、重複、插入或倒過來，這些較大的改變叫結構變異。

Besides single-letter changes, DNA can have large stretches deleted, duplicated, inserted or flipped; these bigger changes are structural variants.

進階Advanced L2

結構變異涵蓋缺失、重複、多等位 CNV、移動元件（Alu、L1）插入與倒位等。1000 Genomes 第三期描述超過 8,800 萬個變異，其中約 60,000 個 SV，並以微陣列與長讀段定序等正交方法確認。

Structural variants include deletions, duplications, multi-allelic CNVs, mobile-element (Alu, L1) insertions and inversions. 1000 Genomes phase 3 described over 88 million variants including ~60,000 SVs, confirmed with orthogonal methods such as microarrays and long-read sequencing.

例子Examples

  • （示意）某段 5 kb 的序列在一條染色體上缺失，造成拷貝數由 2 變成 1。(Illustrative) A 5 kb segment deleted on one chromosome changes the copy number from 2 to 1.
