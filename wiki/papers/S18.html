S18 Clinical use of current polygenic risk scores may exacerbate health disparities

Martin AR, Kanai M, Kamatani Y, et al. · 2019 · journal_article · doi:10.1038/s41588-019-0379-x

研究背景Background

PRS 被期待用於精準醫療。

PRS expected to advance precision medicine.

方法Methods

跨族群分析 PRS 準確度與 GWAS 族群組成。

Cross-ancestry analysis of PRS accuracy and GWAS composition.

主要結果Main Findings

目前 PRS 在歐洲血統中準確數倍；臨床使用可能擴大不平等。

PRS several-fold more accurate in European ancestry; clinical use may widen disparities.

限制Limitations

觀點文章，結論基於現有 GWAS 組成。

Perspective based on current GWAS composition.
