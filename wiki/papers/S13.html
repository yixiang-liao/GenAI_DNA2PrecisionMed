S13 10 Years of GWAS Discovery: Biology, Function, and Translation

Visscher PM, Wray NR, Zhang Q, et al. · 2017 · journal_article · doi:10.1016/j.ajhg.2017.06.005

研究背景Background

GWAS 實施十年的回顧。

Ten years of GWAS reviewed.

方法Methods

綜述族群遺傳、疾病生物學與轉譯成果。

Review of population genetics, disease biology and translation.

主要結果Main Findings

以 p < 5×10⁻⁸ 定義約 10,000 個強關聯；偵測力取決於樣本量、效應分布、頻率與 LD。

~10,000 strong associations at p < 5×10⁻⁸; power depends on sample size, effect distribution, frequency and LD.

限制Limitations

2017 年數據，關聯數量已大幅增加。

2017 figures; association counts have grown greatly.
