S20 Genome-wide polygenic scores for common diseases identify individuals with risk equivalent to monogenic mutations

Khera AV, Chaffin M, Aragam KG, et al. · 2018 · journal_article · doi:10.1038/s41588-018-0183-z

研究背景Background

臨床多聚焦於罕見單基因突變。

Clinical focus had been on rare monogenic mutations.

方法Methods

UK Biobank 英國血統；Phase I 選分數、Phase II 獨立測試。

UK Biobank British ancestry; Phase I selection, Phase II testing.

主要結果Main Findings

8.0%（CAD）至 1.5%（乳癌）人口相較其餘人口勝算比 ≥3；主張考慮納入臨床。

8.0% (CAD) to 1.5% (breast cancer) with an odds ratio ≥3 versus the remainder; proposes clinical consideration.

限制Limitations

單一歐洲血統世代；以勝算比（相對比較）呈現，未提供絕對風險；未評估臨床結果改善。

Single European cohort; results as odds ratios (relative), no absolute risk; no outcome evaluation.
