S25 Standards and guidelines for the interpretation of sequence variants: a joint consensus recommendation of the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology

Richards S, Aziz N, Bale S, et al. · 2015 · journal_article · doi:10.1038/gim.2015.30

研究背景Background

NGS 臨床檢測增加變異判讀複雜度。

Clinical NGS made variant interpretation complex.

方法Methods

ACMG/AMP/CAP 工作小組專家共識。

ACMG/AMP/CAP workgroup expert consensus.

主要結果Main Findings

五級變異分類術語與證據準則；建議 CLIA 實驗室與認證專家判讀。

Five-tier terminology and evidence criteria; CLIA labs and certified interpretation.

限制Limitations

適用孟德爾疾病；不適用常見變異與 PRS。

For Mendelian disease; not for common variants or PRS.
