S03 Coming of age: ten years of next-generation sequencing technologies

Goodwin S, McPherson JD, McCombie WR · 2016 · journal_article · doi:10.1038/nrg.2016.49

研究背景Background

人類基因體計畫後十年的定序技術回顧。

Review of a decade of sequencing after the Human Genome Project.

方法Methods

比較短讀段與長讀段平台的原理、錯誤率與應用。

Compares short- and long-read platforms: chemistry, error rates, applications.

主要結果Main Findings

成本下降、基因體數量增加；短讀段追求通量，長讀段解析結構複雜區域。

Falling cost and more genomes; short reads maximize throughput, long reads resolve complex regions.

限制Limitations

2016 年的技術現況，成本與通量數據已過時。

State of the art as of 2016; cost/throughput figures are dated.
