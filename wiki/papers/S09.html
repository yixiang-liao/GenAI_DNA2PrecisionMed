S09 A survey of best practices for RNA-seq data analysis

Conesa A, Madrigal P, Tarazona S, et al. · 2016 · journal_article · doi:10.1186/s13059-016-0881-8

研究背景Background

RNA-seq 應用多樣，缺乏統一流程。

Diverse RNA-seq uses with no unified pipeline.

方法Methods

回顧設計、QC、比對、定量、差異表現、剪接、功能分析與 eQTL。

Reviews design, QC, alignment, quantification, DE, splicing, functional analysis, eQTL.

主要結果Main Findings

沒有適用所有情境的單一流程；讀段數需正規化才能跨樣本比較。

No single pipeline fits all; counts need normalization for cross-sample comparison.

限制Limitations

2016 年的工具與建議，部分已更新。

Tools and advice as of 2016; some updated since.
