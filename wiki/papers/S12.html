S12 Genome-wide association studies

Uffelmann E, Huang QQ, Munung NS, et al. · 2021 · journal_article · doi:10.1038/s43586-021-00056-9

研究背景Background

GWAS 方法學的系統性入門。

Systematic primer on GWAS methodology.

方法Methods

Nature Reviews Methods Primers 綜述：統計基礎、實施、限制與應用。

Nature Reviews Methods Primers: statistics, conduct, limitations, applications.

主要結果Main Findings

GWAS 檢定大量變異以找出統計關聯；結果用於生物學、遺傳率、風險預測、藥物開發與因果推論。

GWAS test many variants for statistical association; uses span biology, heritability, risk prediction, drug development, causal inference.
