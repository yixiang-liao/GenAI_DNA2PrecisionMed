S11 Moderated estimation of fold change and dispersion for RNA-seq data with DESeq2

Love MI, Huber W, Anders S · 2014 · journal_article · doi:10.1186/s13059-014-0550-8

研究背景Background

計數資料重複數少、離散且有離群值。

Count data with few replicates, discreteness and outliers.

方法Methods

負二項模型加上 dispersion 與 fold change 收縮估計。

Negative binomial model with shrinkage of dispersions and fold changes.

主要結果Main Findings

DESeq2 提供更穩定、可解釋的差異表現估計。

DESeq2 gives stable, interpretable DE estimates.

限制Limitations

假設多數基因非差異表現；極端設計需另行檢查。

Assumes most genes are not DE; unusual designs need checks.
