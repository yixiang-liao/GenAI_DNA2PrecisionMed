S10 Measurement of mRNA abundance using RNA-seq data: RPKM measure is inconsistent among samples

Wagner GP, Kin K, Lynch VJ · 2012 · journal_article · doi:10.1007/s12064-012-0162-3

研究背景Background

RPKM 廣泛用於表現量正規化。

RPKM was widely used for normalization.

方法Methods

理論分析 RPKM 是否保持平均相對莫耳濃度不變。

Theoretical analysis of whether RPKM preserves average relative molar concentration.

主要結果Main Findings

RPKM 在樣本間不一致；提出 TPM。

RPKM is inconsistent among samples; proposes TPM.

限制Limitations

理論論證；TPM 仍為相對量，不能直接用於差異檢定。

Theoretical; TPM is still relative and not a DE test by itself.
