S17 The personal and clinical utility of polygenic risk scores

Torkamani A, Wineinger NE, Topol EJ · 2018 · journal_article · doi:10.1038/s41576-018-0018-x

研究背景Background

GWAS 初期對個人化預測期望很高。

High early expectations of GWAS for personal prediction.

方法Methods

回顧 PRS 個人與臨床效用的證據。

Reviews evidence for personal and clinical PRS utility.

主要結果Main Findings

早期判斷幫助有限；近期證據支持群體層級的風險分層。

Early view: little use; recent evidence supports group-level stratification.

限制Limitations

回顧文章；作者觀點偏向支持 PRS 應用。

Review with a generally favourable stance.
