S08 RNA-Seq: a revolutionary tool for transcriptomics

Wang Z, Gerstein M, Snyder M · 2009 · journal_article · doi:10.1038/nrg2484

研究背景Background

轉錄體分析從微陣列轉向定序。

Transcriptomics moving from microarrays to sequencing.

方法Methods

回顧 RNA-seq 原理、挑戰與早期應用。

Reviews RNA-seq principles, challenges and early applications.

主要結果Main Findings

RNA-seq 能更精確測量轉錄本與 isoform，改變對轉錄體複雜度的認識。

More precise transcript/isoform measurement; changed views of transcriptome complexity.

限制Limitations

2009 年早期回顧，未涵蓋後續分析方法。

Early (2009) review; predates later methods.
